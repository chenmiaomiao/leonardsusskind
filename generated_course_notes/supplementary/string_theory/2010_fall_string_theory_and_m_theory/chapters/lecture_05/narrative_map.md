# Narrative Map

## Opening Question

The lecture asks for the physical scale omitted from the preceding
kinematical discussion. A spring first makes tension concrete; natural units
then show why tension controls spacing in mass squared. This question expands
into Planck units, the experimental remoteness of fundamental-string
excitations, and the obstruction created by gravitational collapse.

## Beat Sequence

1. **Introduce tension.** Hooke's law, the infinite-momentum relation, and
   the rest-frame energy of a stretched string identify energy per length as
   the missing scale.
2. **Read its dimensions.** In natural units tension has dimensions of mass
   squared, so it fixes oscillator and Regge-level gaps.
3. **Construct Planck units.** The dimensions of \(c\), \(\hbar\), and \(G\)
   are combined first into area, then into length, time, mass, and energy.
4. **Compare string and gravity scales.** The lecture treats their proximity
   as a physical expectation, not a dimensional theorem, and tests it against
   running couplings and the absence of observed substructure.
5. **Ask how one would excite a fundamental string.** Accelerator length,
   luminosity, extra dimensions, uncertainty, and black-hole formation make
   direct Planck-scale spectroscopy implausible.
6. **Restart after the break.** The massless open-string vector requires its
   ground state to begin one unit below zero.
7. **Expose the divergent vacuum energy.** Every transverse coordinate
   contributes the half-sum of all positive mode frequencies.
8. **Separate motion from invariant mass.** The large-\(P\) expansion
   explains why internal string energy is read as mass squared after the
   common longitudinal piece is removed.
9. **Regulate the sum.** Exponential damping, a derivative of a geometric
   series, and a small-\(\epsilon\) expansion isolate the finite
   \(-1/24\) contribution per transverse coordinate.
10. **Count dimensions.** Requiring an open-string intercept of \(-1\)
    gives 24 transverse coordinates and hence 26 spacetime dimensions; the
    doubled closed-string oscillator content independently gives \(-2\).
11. **Point toward the deeper reason.** The historical regulator argument is
    explicitly distinguished from conformal-anomaly cancellation and the
    consistent constraint algebra developed next.

## Transition Cues

- The dimensional-analysis act should remain physical: every formula is tied
  to a spectrum, an experiment, or a localization thought experiment.
- The break marks a genuine restart. Do not disguise the shift from scale
  estimates to the critical-dimension calculation.
- The large-momentum expansion belongs before regularization because it
  explains which energy is being renormalized and why mass squared is the
  spectral variable.
- Preserve the regulator's divergent term. The lecture does not claim that
  ordinary summation makes positive integers add to a negative number.
- The closed-string check completes the argument; it is not a second,
  forty-eight-dimensional theory.
