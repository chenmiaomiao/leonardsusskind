## Reconstruction Decisions

- The old incomplete four-vector and interval frames were replaced by
  completed mass-shell, all-incoming, and Mandelstam board states that carry
  more of the actual derivation.
- Physical outgoing momenta are distinguished from their sign-reversed
  all-incoming labels, avoiding the false impression of negative physical
  energy.
- The rough discretized-chain picture is retained and paired with one small
  TikZ reconstruction of two strings joining, propagating for (	au), and
  splitting.
- The live sign corrections in the final integral are represented by the
  mathematically consistent sequence supported by the final board states;
  intermediate false starts are not presented as additional formulas.
- The beta-function arguments use the lecture's units. A labeled note states
  where the standard Regge trajectory functions belong.

## Caption Standard

Captions describe what the visible board state contributes to the argument
rather than guessing at unclear chalk. Every source image carries its lecture
timestamp, and the clean redraw is explicitly identified as an explanatory
companion to the original frame.
