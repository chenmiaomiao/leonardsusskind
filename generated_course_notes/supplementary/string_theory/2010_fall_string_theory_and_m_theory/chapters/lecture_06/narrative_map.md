# Narrative Map

## Opening Question

The lecture asks how an experiment can test a string theory. Scattering first
reduces the observable data to Lorentz-invariant kinematics. Ordinary channel
diagrams then expose a historical puzzle: meson amplitudes seemed to require
many direct and crossed contributions, while the Veneziano function contains
both descriptions at once. The second half reconstructs that function from
open strings that join, propagate, and split.

## Beat Sequence

1. **Clear the stage.** Fermionic strings and ten dimensions are recalled,
   compactification is deferred, and scattering becomes the immediate test.
2. **Define the experimental object.** Incoming data, a black-box collision,
   outgoing data, and a complex amplitude connect theory to observed rates.
3. **Make momentum notation symmetric.** The mostly-plus mass shell and the
   all-incoming convention put all four external legs on equal footing.
4. **Remove redundant variables.** The center-of-mass frame leaves collision
   energy and scattering angle; Lorentz invariance packages them as
   (s,t,u), with (s+t+u=4m^2) for equal masses.
5. **Give channels physical meaning.** A direct resonance loses directional
   memory, while crossed exchange carries momentum transfer and angular
   dependence; a third connectivity gives the (u)-channel.
6. **Present the historical surprise.** Towers of meson resonances motivate
   the Veneziano amplitude, whose one analytic form admits both direct- and
   crossed-channel expansions.
7. **Return from formula to mechanism.** An open string sweeps out a strip,
   obeys a worldsheet wave equation, and can be regulated as masses joined by
   springs.
8. **Construct the interaction.** Two product wavefunctions are restricted
   to endpoint contact, evolved as one joined string for time (	au), and
   projected onto two outgoing strings.
9. **Sum over the unknown lifetime.** Integrating over (	au), then setting
   (z=e^{-\tau}), produces the Euler beta integral and hence the Veneziano
   gamma-function ratio.
10. **Expose the geometric question.** The substitution (z\mapsto1-z)
    demonstrates channel symmetry; conformal symmetry is announced as its
    deeper worldsheet explanation.
11. **Close the physical checks.** Endpoint counting, bosonic versus
    fermionic strings, open versus closed strings, and scattering tests of
    photon and graviton identities complete the lecture, while charge is
    deliberately left for later.

## Transition Cues

- Keep the center-of-mass reduction exploratory: the lecture teaches why
  only two pieces of physical data survive rather than merely stating it.
- Interpret each Mandelstam variable before advancing to diagrams.
- Do not introduce the Veneziano formula before the inadequacy of separately
  added meson channels has created the puzzle it solves.
- Preserve the deliberate rewind from the finished amplitude back to the
  discrete oscillator-chain mechanism.
- Keep the lifetime integral after joining, evolution, and projection; its
  meaning depends on those steps.
- Let conformal symmetry remain a promised explanation, not a completed
  derivation in this chapter.
