## Reconstruction Decisions

- The opening sequence uses completed particle-action and path-integral
  states rather than generic path sketches.
- The Euclidean particle and worldsheet frames were chosen after the live
  sign corrections, so the screenshots agree with the typeset positive
  actions.
- The finite-difference sequence retains three successive board states:
  one-dimensional second differences, the two-dimensional grid, and the
  completed mean-value equation.
- The conformal-map section keeps both the original level-curve drawing and
  a small clean schematic.  The schematic clarifies the map but does not
  replace the lecture evidence.
- The final two frames are deliberately ordered by time: channel geometries
  first, momentum labels second.

## Caption Standard

Captions identify only what is visibly present and how it functions in the
argument.  Each source image carries its exact lecture timestamp.  Claims
about channel identity that depend on speech rather than chalk are made in
the prose, not smuggled into the image description.
