3. Derive signed winding conservation from opposite- and same-orientation reconnection pictures.

6. Reset after the break. Apply T-duality to open-string endpoint conditions so Neumann becomes Dirichlet and a D-brane is required.
7. Count the D\(p\)-brane dimensions, distinguish D-strings from fundamental strings, and build low-energy gauge theories from open strings on brane stacks.
8. End with D-string endpoints as magnetic monopoles and with the lecture's historical return from hadronic strings to gauge theory.
