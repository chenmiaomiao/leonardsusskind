# Visual Validation

## Selection Standard

Candidate windows were inspected around every equation and interaction beat.
The retained frame had to show the completed relevant board state, match the
transcript timestamp, and remain legible at publication size. Frames were
rejected when the writing was unfinished, hidden by the lecturer, or unrelated
to the surrounding prose.

## Board Sequence

The 22 retained frames form one continuous visual argument:

1. oscillator Hamiltonian and ladder normalization;
2. transverse coordinate operators and the open-string field;
3. spin counting and circular polarization;
4. ground and first excited open-string levels;
5. group velocity, Klein--Gordon dynamics, and field energy;
6. coupled-pendulum instability;
7. rotation, joining, self-closing, closed fusion, and mixed interactions.

## Equation Checks

- The one-quarter mode normalization gives \(p_n=\dot X_n/2\) and
  \(H_n=p_n^2+n^2X_n^2/4\).
- The normalized operators obey \([a_n,a_m^\dagger]=\delta_{nm}\), so one
  mode-\(n\) quantum raises the level by \(n\).
- The two first-level states carry helicities \(+1\) and \(-1\); there is no
  third state for a massive vector.
- Negative mass squared is checked through
  \(\omega^2=k^2-\mu^2\), including the exponentially growing low-\(k\)
  branch.
- The interaction redraws preserve endpoint joining, self-closing, closed
  reconnection, and closed-string absorption without adding unsupported
  vertices.

## Redraw Policy

Six clean redraws clarify geometry that is intentionally rough on the board.
Each is placed beside or immediately after its source frame. The original
frame remains the evidence; the redraw is only a legibility aid.
