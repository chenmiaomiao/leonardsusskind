## Source Arc

The 1:55:56 lecture has two promised results and one continuous argument.
It begins with the permissiveness of point-particle mechanics on curved
spaces, turns the zero-point size of a string into a cutoff problem, and
uses the sphere to show that curvature couples internal string structure to
long-distance motion.  Generalizing the shrinking-sphere argument produces
a metric flow controlled at leading order by the Ricci tensor.  A stable
background is Ricci flat, which is also the vacuum Einstein condition.

The second half asks what to do with the extra dimensions required by
worldsheet consistency.  Cylinders and periodic face identifications build
up the intrinsic picture of flat tori.  The circle compactification then
supplies Kaluza--Klein momentum modes.  Closed strings add winding modes,
and the exchange of the two towers under radius inversion gives T-duality.
The winding-pair argument closes the remaining loophole: winding sectors
cannot be removed consistently.
