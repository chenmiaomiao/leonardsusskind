# Visual Validation

## Selection Standard

Each retained image was extracted from the Lecture 2 recording at the point where its equation or diagram is being developed. Frames were rejected when the board was obscured, the formula was unfinished, the image showed only the Stanford opening, or the visible material belonged to a different narrative beat.

## Board Sequence

The 23 retained frames form one continuous visual argument:

1. calculus on \([0,\pi]\);
2. endpoint conditions and Fourier bases;
3. orthogonality and oscillator normalization;
4. the spectral definition of particle behavior;
5. the large-momentum Hamiltonian;
6. the discrete and continuum open string;
7. the endpoint derivation of Neumann data;
8. normal-mode diagonalization and quantization;
9. open-string self-joining into a closed string.

## Equation Checks

- The continuum string uses both transverse coordinates \(X\) and \(Y\), with the same normalization in the energy and Lagrangian.
- The right-end force has the restoring sign \(F_{\mathrm{end}}=-(1/\pi)\partial_\sigma X\); the sign reverses at the left end.
- The zero cosine mode has a different norm and is treated as center-of-mass translation, not an internal oscillator.
- Differentiating a cosine mode supplies both the minus sign and the factor \(n\); sine orthogonality then yields \(\omega_n=n\).
- The level-two count is five: three two-quantum states of the first harmonic and two one-quantum states of the second harmonic.

## Redraw Policy

Six schematic redraws clarify boundary profiles, the spectral gap, the bead chain, endpoint dynamics, oscillator multiplicities, and open-to-closed joining. They are positioned beside the corresponding original frame and do not introduce unsupported board content.
