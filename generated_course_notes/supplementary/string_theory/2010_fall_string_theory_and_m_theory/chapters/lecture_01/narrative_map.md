# Narrative Map

## Through-Line

The chapter follows one continuous argument. Hadron spectroscopy first reveals nearly linear, parallel Regge trajectories. Pion scattering then reveals channel duality, suggesting that resonance production and particle exchange are two slicings of one world sheet. Modern QCD interprets the spatial slice as a confined flux tube. The infinite-momentum frame finally supplies a manageable transverse Hamiltonian, and its identification with mass squared converts Hooke-law stretching into constant rest-frame tension.

## Source Order

1. **Hadronic origin, 00:00:07--00:11:31.** Quarks and gluons were not yet settled experimental language. Chew--Frautschi plots, the two-point historical joke, later confirming states, universal Regge slope, basketball and atom endpoints, and the contrast with electrons establish the need for an extended hadron.
2. **Dual scattering channels, 00:11:31--00:20:02.** Pion annihilation through a rho tower and crossed-channel exchange numerically reproduce one another. Quark-line topology turns the apparent overcounting into two readings of one surface, whose spatial slice is a string.
3. **QCD and quantum gravity, 00:20:02--00:35:21.** Audience questions test the string interpretation. Confined gluon flux explains stretchability; hadronic and Planck scales are separated; the unwanted massless spin-two state motivates the quantum-gravity reinterpretation; world-sheet language and Lorentz slicing are fixed.
4. **Relativistic kinematics, 00:35:21--00:57:05.** The ordinary small-momentum expansion is contrasted with a large-\(P_z\) expansion. Conserved longitudinal momentum is removed, inverse-\(P_z\) time dilation is explained, and \(P_z\) becomes transverse inertia while the mass-squared term becomes internal energy.
5. **Open-string mechanics, 00:57:05--01:14:44.** Beads and springs regulate the string. Bead masses vanish and spring constants grow in the continuum limit; \(\sigma\) runs from \(0\) to \(\pi\); the Hamiltonian contains transverse kinetic and stretching terms. Center-of-mass motion is separated from internal motion, whose Hamiltonian is proportional to \(M^2\).
6. **Tension and confinement, 01:14:44--01:42:04.** Uniform stretching gives \(H_{\mathrm{int}}\propto L^2\), hence \(M\propto L\) and \(E_{\mathrm{rest}}=TL\). The two energy expansions are repeated after the break. A superconducting flux-line thought experiment supplies an explicit linear-confinement analogue and motivates the dual-superconductor picture of QCD.
7. **Degrees of freedom and scale, 01:42:04--01:46:47.** The final exchange explains why a tiny electron can lack accessible rotational excitations while hadrons, atoms, and basketballs occupy progressively lower excitation scales.

## Voice And Pacing

- Retain the post-break repetition: the paired expansions repair the easiest place to confuse \(M\) with \(M^2\).
- Keep genuine audience questions beside the arguments they test.
- Mark only the modern Regge notation, exact light-cone invariant relation, and status of the dual-superconductor picture as editorial clarifications.
