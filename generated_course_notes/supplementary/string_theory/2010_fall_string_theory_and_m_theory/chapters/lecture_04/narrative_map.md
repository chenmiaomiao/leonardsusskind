# Narrative Map

## Opening Setup

The lecture deliberately begins with a piece of canonical mechanics whose
purpose is not immediately announced. Momentum contracted with an
infinitesimal displacement gives the generator of a symmetry. That fact is
kept in reserve until shifting the coordinate origin of a closed string turns
out to impose the physical-state condition.

## Beat Sequence

1. **Recall the generator.** Canonical momentum and a Noether displacement
   produce the conserved charge, with rotation as the concrete example.
2. **Put a coordinate on a loop.** A marked origin and intrinsic orientation
   define sigma without making the mark physical or confusing intrinsic
   direction with clockwise motion in the room.
3. **Expand periodic waves.** Closed-string coordinates use integer Fourier
   modes, with two independently propagating families and a center-of-mass
   zero mode.
4. **Split the energy.** Completing the square isolates the two chiral
   sectors and explains why closed strings have twice the oscillator content
   of open strings.
5. **Make the wrong spectrum guess.** One-sided first excitations look like
   two photons but fail to assemble into sensible rotational multiplets.
6. **Impose level matching.** Equal left- and right-moving levels remove the
   one-sided states and retain four bilinears at the first physical level.
7. **Read the surviving particles.** Circular polarization exposes helicity
   plus and minus two, while the two helicity-zero combinations become the
   dilaton and antisymmetric tensor, or its four-dimensional axion dual.
8. **Derive the rule.** Cyclic relabeling of a discretized loop becomes
   invariance under a common sigma shift; its generator is the difference of
   the two chiral energies.
9. **Separate two meanings of handedness.** World-sheet propagation and
   spacetime circular polarization are independent labels.
10. **Broaden the meaning of elementary.** Dirac quantization makes electric
    and magnetic descriptions exchange weak and strong coupling, preparing
    the same change of viewpoint for strings, D-branes, and M-theory.

## Transition Cues

- The Noether formula should feel temporarily unmotivated, then become the
  decisive bridge in the level-matching derivation.
- The spectrum argument must preserve the failed one-sided guess before
  presenting the constraint; otherwise the reason for the rule disappears.
- The board correction in the helicity calculation belongs in the narrative,
  because it distinguishes world-sheet direction from transverse helicity.
- The late discussion of fundamentality is a conceptual coda, not a separate
  essay. Its function is to turn strong-weak duality into the entrance to
  M-theory.

## Pacing Risks

- Do not flatten intrinsic orientation into clockwise versus
  counterclockwise motion.
- Do not assign propagation direction from the sign in a spatial Fourier
  factor before restoring its time phase.
- Do not state level matching as an unexplained textbook axiom only; retain
  the discrete relabeling and continuum generator argument.
- Do not identify the generic antisymmetric tensor directly with a scalar in
  every dimension; the axion language is the four-dimensional dual.
- Do not turn the operational discussion of fundamentality into a claim that
  every description is equally useful at every coupling.
