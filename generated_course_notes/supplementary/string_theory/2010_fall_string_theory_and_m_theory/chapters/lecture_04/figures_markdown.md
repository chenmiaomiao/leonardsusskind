## Reconstruction Decisions

- Blackboard superscripts `+` and subscripts `+n,-n` are translated into
  dagger and tilde notation only after the board convention is stated.
- The helicity products use one operator from each world-sheet sector. This
  preserves the correction made during class and prevents a one-sided state
  from being mistaken for a physical closed-string excitation.
- The wavefunction's cyclic relabeling is not generalized to arbitrary
  permutations; adjacency around the loop must remain intact.
- Ordinary derivative-looking board notation is upgraded to a functional
  derivative only where the continuum wavefunctional requires it.
- Overall normalization in the level-matching generator is left proportional
  when the board convention is ambiguous. Its vanishing is normalization
  independent.

## Caption Standard

Captions identify the physical role of each board state rather than claim a
literal transcription of every chalk mark. Timestamps are attached to the
figure environment, never inserted as free-standing prose.
