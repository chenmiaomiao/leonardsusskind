Revised and ready for course assembly.

## Initial Findings

- The old chapter compressed a 95-minute lecture into a short account that stopped before most of the neutrino, heavy-particle, cosmic-ray, inertia, gravity, and historical Higgs discussion.
- Forty recoverable classroom exchanges test the gauge-mass recap, helicity and chirality, the Dirac equations, Yukawa invariance, beam polarization, Majorana mass, relativistic kinematics, gravity, and antiparticles.

- The original frame set ended at the first Dirac split and did not preserve the central Yukawa, Higgs-fluctuation, or species-dependent mass equations.

- Marked modern Higgs, neutrino, dark-matter, and lepton-number clarifications as editorial notes rather than blending them into the 2010 lecture.
