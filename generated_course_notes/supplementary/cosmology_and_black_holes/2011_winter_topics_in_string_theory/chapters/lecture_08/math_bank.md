# Mathematics Bank

## Quantum Vacuum

- \(E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega\)
- \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\rm vac}=-\rho_{\rm vac}g_{\mu\nu}\)
- \(p_{\rm vac}=-\rho_{\rm vac}\)
- \(\dot\rho+3H(\rho+p)=0\)

## Expanding Geometry

- \(ds^2=-dt^2+a(t)^2d\mathbf{x}^2\)
- \(D(t)=a(t)\lvert\Delta\mathbf{x}\rvert\)
- \(H(t)=\dot a/a\)
- \(H^2=(8\pi G_N/3)\rho\)
- \(\rho_m=\rho_0a^{-3}\)
- \(\rho_r\propto a^{-4}\)

## Matter Solution

\[
\dot a^2=K/a,\qquad
a^{1/2}da=\sqrt K\,dt,\qquad
\frac23a^{3/2}=\sqrt K\,(t-t_B),
\]
\[
a(t)\propto(t-t_B)^{2/3}.
\]

## Vacuum Solution

\[
\rho_{\rm vac}=\text{constant},\qquad
H^2=\frac{8\pi G_N}{3}\rho_{\rm vac},
\]
\[
\dot a=Ha,\qquad
a(t)=Ce^{Ht},\qquad
D_H=c/H.
\]

## de Sitter Conformal Coordinates

\[
ds^2=-dt^2+e^{2Ht}d\mathbf{x}^2,
\qquad
T=-H^{-1}e^{-Ht},
\]
\[
e^{2Ht}=\frac{1}{H^2T^2},
\qquad
ds^2=\frac{1}{H^2T^2}(-dT^2+d\mathbf{x}^2).
\]

For null rays,
\[
ds^2=0,\qquad dT=\pm dx.
\]
The horizon's comoving and proper radii are
\[
\Delta x_H=-T=\frac{e^{-Ht}}{H},
\qquad
D_H=a\Delta x_H=\frac1H.
\]
