# Visual Notes

## Selection Method

Candidate frames were inspected in adjacent timestamp windows rather than accepted from a fixed interval. The chosen instant had to show the equation or diagram named in the caption, avoid unrelated opening material, and minimize obstruction by the lecturer.

## Visual Sequence

1. Geometry enters through \(G_{\mu\nu}\) and \(g_{\mu\nu}\).
2. The flat expanding metric and Friedmann equation establish the cosmological system.
3. Successive frames preserve the matter substitution, integration, and decelerating graph.
4. A clean board state records \(\dot a=Ha\) and \(a=e^{Ht}\).
5. The mixed-era graph and force sketch translate the equations into cosmic history.
6. Flat and black-hole Penrose diagrams define a horizon before de Sitter is analyzed.
7. Four adjacent frames retain the conformal-time derivation, conformal factor, metric, and null-ray rule.
8. Alice and Bob diagrams show observer dependence and loss of causal contact.
9. The final frames support the redshift analogy and the fixed proper horizon radius.

## Layout

Frames are centered and kept below text width. Captions identify only visible, source-confirmed content. Dense causal diagrams are explained immediately before or after the image, and no screenshot substitutes for a typeset derivation.
