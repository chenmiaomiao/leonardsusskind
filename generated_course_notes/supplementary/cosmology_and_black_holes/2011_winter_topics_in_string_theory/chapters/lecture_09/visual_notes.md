# Visual Notes

## Selection Method

Candidate frames were sampled on both sides of each transcript timestamp. A frame was retained only when the active equation or diagram was complete, minimally obstructed, and semantically matched to the surrounding paragraph. Captions describe visible content without assigning claims that the board does not support.

## Layout

All 17 source frames are centered below the argument they document. Dense equations are retypeset in the prose, so no conclusion depends on reading chalk handwriting. Image widths are chosen to keep captions and closing arguments together; the final frame is reduced slightly to avoid an orphaned closing paragraph.
