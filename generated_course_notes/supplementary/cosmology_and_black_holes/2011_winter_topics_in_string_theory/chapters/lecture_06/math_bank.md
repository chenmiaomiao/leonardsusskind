# Math Bank

## Lecture Relations

- \(\ell_p=\sqrt{G_N\hbar/c^3}\), followed by \(c=\hbar=1\).
- \(G_N=\ell_p^2\).
- \(F_{\mathrm{grav}}\sim G_NM_1M_2/r^2\).
- \(G_N\sim g_s^2\ell_s^2\), hence \(\ell_p\sim g_s\ell_s\), in the simplified four-dimensional setup.
- \(S_{\mathrm{BH}}=A/(4G_N)\sim A/\ell_p^2\sim M^2\ell_p^2\).
- \(\Omega_n\sim4^n\), \(S_{\mathrm{st}}\sim n\log4\sim n\).
- \(n=L/\ell_s\), where \(L\) is contour length rather than spatial diameter.
- \(m_{\mathrm{link}}\sim1/\ell_s\) and \(M\sim L/\ell_s^2\).
- \(S_{\mathrm{st}}\sim M\ell_s\).
- \(R_s\sim\ell_s\) at the correspondence point.
- \(Mg_s^2\ell_s\sim1\) at that point.
- \(S_{\mathrm{BH}}\sim(M\ell_s)(Mg_s^2\ell_s)\sim M\ell_s\sim S_{\mathrm{st}}\).
- \(\Delta S=0\) along the slow reversible correspondence path.

## Precision Notes

- Use \(g_s\) for the dimensionless string coupling and \(G_N\) for Newton's constant.
- A splitting or joining amplitude carries \(g_s\); a leading probability or rate carries \(g_s^2\).
- Numerical factors \(2\), \(4\), and \(\pi\) are suppressed whenever \(\sim\) is used.
- The effective relation for \(G_N\) depends on dimension and compactification data; only its lecture-level scaling is used.
- A string tension \(T_s\sim1/\ell_s^2\) explains \(m_{\mathrm{link}}\sim T_s\ell_s\sim1/\ell_s\).
- The random-walk count captures exponential state growth but omits physical-state constraints and order-one coefficients.
- The entropy match is parametric. Exact protected counts require more specialized black-hole systems.
- ``Adiabatic'' is interpreted as isolated, quasistatic, and reversible; an irreversible adiabatic process can produce entropy.
