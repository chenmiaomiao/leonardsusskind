# Visual Evidence

The final figure set was selected from adjacent video windows, not from isolated keyframes. Each accepted image has a semantic match in the nearby transcript and is placed next to the corresponding chapter argument.

The sequence has four visual phases:

1. Bowl, inverted hat, and selected vacuum point.
2. Standard Model inventory, emission vertices, dipole splitting, and photon condensate.
3. Chiral mixing, weak-boson mass, and the radial Higgs mode.
4. Fermion decay, gluon-fusion production, and diphoton decay.

The source frames remain intentionally documentary. Equations are typeset separately so rough handwriting is not mistaken for exact notation. The full board is retained where spatial relationships matter, and no crop removes the active equation or diagram.

All 13 frames were checked at full resolution. The final PDF uses their original 16:9 aspect ratio, keeps every caption adjacent to its image, and records the source timestamp immediately below the caption.
