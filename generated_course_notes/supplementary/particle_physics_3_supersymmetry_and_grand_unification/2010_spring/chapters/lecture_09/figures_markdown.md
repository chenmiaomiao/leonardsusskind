## Blackboard Reading

### Figure 02

The upper board gives the chiral coordinate and component expansion:

\[
y^\mu=x^\mu+i\bar\theta\bar\sigma^\mu\theta,
\qquad
\Phi=\phi+\psi\theta+F\theta\theta.
\]

The lower line collects the scalar kinetic term, the Weyl-fermion kinetic term, and \(F^\ast F\). Exact normalizations and spinor indices are restored in the chapter rather than inferred character by character from the low-resolution board.

### Figure 03

The frame records

\[
\int d^2\theta\,\Phi^n+\mathrm{c.c.}
\;\leadsto\;
nF\phi^{n-1}
+\frac{n(n-1)}2\phi^{n-2}\psi\psi
+\mathrm{c.c.}
\]

up to convention-dependent signs. The visible arrow is an extraction arrow: the lecturer explicitly explains that it does not equate the full superfield product to its selected component.

### Figure 04

The board groups

\[
\mathcal L_F
\sim
F W'(\phi)+F^\ast\overline{W'(\phi)}+F^\ast F
\]

and begins the equation \(W'(\phi)+F^\ast=0\). The accepted frame is later than the original candidate because the complete line has been written and the lecturer no longer covers it.

### Figure 05

The left board contains a schematic potential with a minimum above or at zero and a boxed derivative condition. The right board preserves the positive-square relation and conjugate \(F\)-equations. The chapter uses the image as documentary evidence but gives the cleaned equations separately because the camera angle and board reuse make individual conjugation marks uncertain.

## Pedagogical Diagrams

Two TikZ diagrams are added from the lecture's own argument:

- a schematic Zeeman-level splitting, which translates rotational degeneracy into separated energies;
- a paired potential sketch, contrasting a zero-energy \(F=0\) minimum with a lifted \(F\neq0\) minimum.

These are explanatory reconstructions, not claimed blackboard facsimiles. The \(SU(5)\) block embedding is expressed by matrices rather than an invented board image.

## Caption Policy

Captions state what is visibly supported, identify replacement timestamps, and avoid claiming that a partly legible board symbol settles a convention. Cleaned formulas use \(W\) for the superpotential and \(V\) for ordinary potential energy throughout.
