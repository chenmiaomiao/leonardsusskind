## Initial Findings

- The previous chapter reduced a 116-minute source to roughly 2,600 words and represented only two generic classroom questions, omitting the extended sign discussion and nearly the entire closing exchange.
- The exploratory fermion action mixed orderings, factors of two, and real versus complex variables without a stable variational convention.
- The statement that conserved supercharges label states like ordinary numerical charges overstated the observable status of odd nilpotent generators.
- The supercharge and covariant-derivative formulas mixed incompatible signs, so the displayed anticommutators and chiral solution could not all hold simultaneously.
- The claim that an ordinary time derivative of a superfield is not a superfield was mathematically false; the actual subtlety concerns bare odd derivatives.
- The conjugate chiral-field calculation was explicitly abandoned at the board and could not support a silently completed component derivation.
- The closing claim about absent bosonic self-energy required qualification: supersymmetry enforces specific cancellations and nonrenormalization relations, not universal vanishing of every correction.
