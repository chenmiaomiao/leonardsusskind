## Reading Decisions

- Frames 03--08 and 10--16 are equation witnesses. Their mathematics is retypeset beside each image because several board lines are intermediate or partially obscured.
- Frame 09 is the only geometric sketch. It is retained directly because the vertical projection, transverse plane, and tilted spin direction are all visible and agree with the nearby statement \(\dot s_3=0\).
- The live sign corrections in the stationary-state and precession calculations are resolved in favor of the final equations accepted in the classroom.
- The spoken claim of "three energy levels" is not reproduced. Frame 13 and the surrounding derivation support two distinct levels: a threefold-degenerate triplet and a nondegenerate singlet.
- The final sine--cosine form is reconstructed from the exact exponential amplitudes. Frame 16 verifies the mean-energy and energy-difference organization, while the algebra fixes the missing signs and factors.
