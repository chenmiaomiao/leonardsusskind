# Lecture Analysis

## Mathematical Spine

The lecture uses three systems to move from Lagrangian technique to
Hamiltonian geometry:

1. build the simple pendulum from coordinates, velocities, and energies;
2. classify its motion by the conserved energy;
3. use the double pendulum to show that the same recipe survives complicated
   coupled motion;
4. expose rotational symmetry by removing gravity and derive its Noether
   charge;
5. recover the harmonic oscillator as the universal approximation near a
   stable minimum;
6. replace velocity by canonical momentum and interpret the oscillator as
   motion on phase-space ellipses.
