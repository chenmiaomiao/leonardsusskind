## Rhythm

The chapter preserves the lecture's deliberate alternation between abstraction
and calculation:

1. Ask what structure phase space carries.
2. State the algebra before using the derivative definition.
3. Derive differentiation from simple monomials.
4. Recover ordinary free motion from the bracket algebra.
5. Test proposed coordinate changes with concrete examples.
6. Replace case-by-case tests with a generator.
7. Read one vanishing bracket as both symmetry and conservation.
8. End with angular momentum and circular motion.
