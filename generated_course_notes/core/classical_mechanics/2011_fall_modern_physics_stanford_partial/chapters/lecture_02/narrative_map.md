# Narrative Map

1. **Use the wrong law to expose order.** The false equation \(F=m\dot x\) lets position determine velocity and every higher derivative. This makes the contrast with Newton structural rather than merely historical.
2. **Recover Newtonian initial data.** Under \(F=m\ddot x\), position fixes acceleration but not velocity. Position and velocity must therefore be supplied independently.
3. **Review energy without mistaking proof for principle.** Friction and the circular-force counterexample motivate conservative forces. Separate derivatives of \(T\) and \(U\) are assembled only at the end into \(F_i-F_i\).
4. **Turn force into momentum flow.** Writing \(\mathbf F=\dot{\mathbf p}\) exposes force-free conservation, universal Newtonian time, and the cancellation of internal forces in a closed system.
5. **Let the classroom interruption widen the theory.** Instantaneous action and reaction belong to Newtonian physics, but momentum conservation survives relativity. Three momentum laws and one energy law point beyond the toy derivation.
6. **Prepare stationarity in finite dimensions.** Minima, maxima, and flat inflection points all have vanishing first derivative. A two-variable quadratic makes the many-variable condition concrete.
7. **Change the object being varied.** Generalized coordinates form a complete trajectory \(\mathbf q(t)\). Newton advances locally from initial data; a variational principle compares whole paths between fixed endpoints.
8. **Learn functionals through geometry and optics.** Arc length, Fermat travel time, and hanging-chain energy each assign a number to an entire curve.
9. **Reveal the surprising sign.** The naive \(\int(T+U)\,dt\) is rejected in favor of \(A=\int(T-U)\,dt\). The minus sign is left to the forthcoming equation-of-motion derivation.
10. **Stop at the correct threshold.** The lecture defines \(L(q,\dot q)\) and the action but postpones the Euler--Lagrange equations until Lecture 3.

The chapter should retain this sequence. In particular, the geometric examples are not optional decoration: they make the final action formula intelligible.
