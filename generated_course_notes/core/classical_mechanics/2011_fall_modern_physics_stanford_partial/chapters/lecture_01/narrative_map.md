# Narrative Map

## Movement 1: Remove Everything Except Evolution

Begin with the claim that classical mechanics supplies the architecture of physics, then replace continuous time by stroboscopic beats. The coin introduces phase space and the operational definition of a state: exactly the information needed to determine the next step.

## Movement 2: Reversibility Appears as Arrow Counting

The identity and flip rules lead to six-state cycles, disconnected cycles, and forbidden merging or branching maps. Audience attempts to erase or terminate arrows sharpen the criterion to one incoming and one outgoing arrow at every state.

## Movement 3: Orbits Carry Memory

Finite systems recur, infinite chains need not, and disconnected components support conserved labels. Reversibility becomes information conservation: distinct exact histories never collapse into the same state.

## Movement 4: The State Must Grow With the Law

A two-step coin rule reveals that a visible symbol need not be a complete state. The same issue in continuous motion requires both position and velocity, producing a two-dimensional phase space for a particle moving on a line.

## Movement 5: Exact Laws Meet Inexact Data

Real-number initial conditions cannot be measured exactly. Error amplification and chaos limit practical forecasts without changing the uniqueness of exact classical trajectories.

## Movement 6: Equation Order Counts Initial Data

The phony first-order law lets position determine velocity and all higher derivatives. Newton's second-order law instead requires position and velocity independently, becoming a first-order flow on the enlarged phase space.

## Movement 7: The Classroom Builds the Missing State

The late return to the coin distinguishes state values from pieces of historical data. After several failed tables, the class finds an XOR rule on the four ordered pairs. The fixed point and three-cycle make reversibility visible and prepare the final observation about higher-order equations.

## Voice and Pacing

- Let each question expose a precise failure in the current definition of state.
- Preserve the recursive return from continuous motion to the coin; it resolves rather than repeats the main issue.
- Keep board evidence next to exact typeset mathematics and state visual limits in captions.
- Use standard mathematical clarification only where it completes an argument already made in the lecture.
