# Visual Notes: Lecture 7

## Selection Result

Fifteen source frames cover the complete visual spine of the lecture:
Hamilton's equations, energy surfaces, phase-space flow, one-dimensional
compression, divergence, reversible-state analogies, the Liouville proof,
shape and topology, damping, and Poisson brackets.

## Interpretation Rules

- Original frames are retained wherever the board layout or drawn geometry
  contributes evidence.
- Clean equations are typeset independently, so partial handwriting does not
  determine notation or signs.
- The harmonic-oscillator portrait is redrawn to show clockwise tangent flow.
- The H=pq board convention is normalized dimensionally in the prose.
- The topology sketches are treated as intuition for invertible smooth flow,
  not as a separate unproved volume theorem.

## Final PDF Review

The compiled 20-page PDF was rendered at review resolution and checked as a
full contact sheet and as enlarged key pages. Frames preserve aspect ratio,
captions remain attached, equations fit the text block, and the title credits
the source lectures and curation without presenting the notes as Susskind's
original manuscript.
