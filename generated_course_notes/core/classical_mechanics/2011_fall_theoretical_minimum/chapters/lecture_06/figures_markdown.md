## Equation Reading

The wedge frames support the velocity decomposition, full Lagrangian, and
canonical momenta. The double-pendulum frames establish the geometry used for
the plus-sign convention in theta + alpha. The Hamiltonian frames show the
adapted oscillator, the differential calculation, and the resulting
first-order equations. Typeset equations remain authoritative wherever the
chalkboard is incomplete or locally inconsistent.

## Uncertainties Resolved

- The board briefly gives the wrong sign for the wedge force; the chapter
  derives the corrected sign from L=T-V.
- The double-pendulum board mixes relative-angle conventions; the text fixes
  one convention and applies it consistently.
- The static phase portrait does not by itself determine circulation
  direction; the Hamilton equations do.

Every selected frame was read in context and placed beside the corresponding
derivation. No campus title card or unrelated transitional frame is used.
