# Visual Notes: Lecture 6

## Selection Result

Fourteen frames were selected from the source video. They cover the two
constrained examples, the reversibility diagrams, the oscillator Legendre
transform, constant-energy curves, Hamilton's-equation derivation, coordinate
rescaling, and phase-space flow. Each frame was checked at its recorded
timestamp against both the transcript and the finished paragraph in which it
appears.

## Reconstruction Guidance

The original frame is retained whenever the chalkboard geometry contributes
evidence. Equations are also typeset in the text so that legibility does not
depend on handwriting. The double-pendulum and wedge sign inconsistencies are
not reproduced as authoritative formulas; their corrected versions follow
from the declared coordinate conventions.

## Final PDF Review

The compiled 17-page PDF was rendered page by page. The figures preserve their
aspect ratios, captions remain with their images, question blocks are readable,
and no equation or paragraph crosses the page boundary. The title and credits
identify the source lectures and curation without presenting the reconstructed
notes as an original manuscript by Leonard Susskind.
