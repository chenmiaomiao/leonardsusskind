# Chapter Plan

## Lecture Arc

- The opening question, “what is acting on what?”, is the genuine doorway. The field equation is rebuilt from scalar-field energy rather than interpreted after the fact.
- The derivation deliberately occurs twice. A fixed box gives \(\ddot\phi=-V'(\phi)\); multiplying the Lagrangian density by the expanding volume \(a^3(t)\) produces \(3H\dot\phi\).
- Hubble friction is made physical through the rock-in-honey analogy and terminal velocity. The Friedmann equation then completes the feedback loop: \(V'\) moves the field while \(V\) drives expansion.
- Inflation is motivated only after the dynamics are established. It dilutes magnetic monopoles, smooths geometry, and requires roughly sixty \(e\)-folds under the standard assumptions.
- The plateau must end. Its potential energy becomes field motion, particles, and heat during reheating, establishing the hot Big Bang while avoiding renewed monopole production.
- A long classroom exchange tests the picture against vacuum energy, the unknown potential shape, field-space time evolution, future horizons, and the information available to distant-future observers.
- The closing caustic calculation addresses the new tension: inflation smooths the universe, yet a small initial velocity field can later form sheets, filaments, knots, and voids. The quantum origin of that initial variation is deferred.

## Mathematical Spine

- Canonical homogeneous density: \(\rho_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2+V(\phi)\).
- Fixed-box Lagrangian: \(\mathcal L_\phi=\dot\phi^2/2-V(\phi)\).
- Expanding-box Lagrangian: \(L_\phi=a^3(\dot\phi^2/2-V)\).
- Field equation: \(\ddot\phi+3H\dot\phi=-V'(\phi)\).
- Slow-roll estimate: \(3H\dot\phi\simeq-V'(\phi)\).
- Friedmann coupling: \(H^2=(8\pi G/3)(\dot\phi^2/2+V)\).
- Potential domination: \(H^2\simeq8\pi GV/3\), hence \(a\propto e^{Ht}\).
- Expansion count: \(N=\int H\,dt\), with \(N\gtrsim60\) as a model-dependent lower-bound estimate.
- Caustic map: \(y=x+v(x)t\), \(dy/dx=1+v'(x)t\), and \(dn/dy=[1+v'(x)t]^{-1}\).

## Physics Corrections And Limits

- The full scalar density contains \(\lvert\nabla\phi\rvert^2/(2a^2)\); it is omitted only in the homogeneous limit.
- Local stress-energy remains covariantly conserved even though a comoving scalar subsystem has no ordinary time-translation conserved energy.
- No particular inflaton or unique potential has been detected. Inflationary evidence is indirect and does not determine the microscopic model.
- Higgs inflation requires additional structure; the argument does not identify the inflaton with the Standard Model Higgs.
- Monopole masses and reheating temperatures are theory-dependent. Classroom numbers remain rough scales.
- A Hubble radius equals the event-horizon scale only in exact de Sitter space; quoted horizon distances are qualitative.
- Today's dark energy need not be the residual value of the primordial inflaton potential.
- The force-free caustic model is geometric intuition, not a replacement for gravitational structure formation.
