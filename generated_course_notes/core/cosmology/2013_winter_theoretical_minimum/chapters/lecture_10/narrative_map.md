# Narrative Map

## Movement 1: Scale Before Cosmology

The lecture delays cosmology in order to establish its units. The contrast between many particle parameters and three universal constants leads naturally into dimensional analysis. Dust, fuel, accelerators, nuclei, the Higgs, dark matter, and black holes make the enormous Planck hierarchy tangible rather than merely numerical.

## Movement 2: The Sky Sets the Target

The WMAP oval is first explained as a map of directions on a last-scattering shell. Near isotropy creates the puzzle, while \(10^{-5}\) anisotropy and approximate scale invariance specify what the theory must produce. Audience questions clarify that these are enormous regions at decoupling, not ordinary microscopic noise made at that epoch.

## Movement 3: An Oscillator Learns to Freeze

Zero-point motion establishes that a ground state is never perfectly still. Viscous damping then separates underdamped, critical, overdamped, and force-free behavior. The decisive limiting solution is a decaying transient plus a constant: motion dies without forcing the displacement back to zero.

## Movement 4: Every Field Mode Is That Oscillator

The inflaton wave equation acquires Hubble friction and the proper-distance factor \(a^{-2}\). Fourier decomposition turns the field into independent oscillators with \(\gamma=3H\) and \(\omega_k=k/a\). Expansion lowers each frequency until its physical wavelength reaches the Hubble scale and its amplitude freezes.

## Movement 5: Frozen Noise Becomes Matter Structure

The homogeneous field still rolls toward the end of inflation, but the frozen perturbation makes neighboring positions arrive at different times. The marching-rank and cliff picture converts a field displacement into a reheating delay. Earlier regions dilute longer, later regions stay denser, and successive wavelengths make nested pockets across many scales.

## Movement 6: One Observable Sky

The last-scattering shell samples that primordial three-dimensional pattern, while its interior evolves into the cosmic web. The closing questions distinguish recombination from inflation and explain why low multipoles are statistically awkward: small patterns repeat across the sky, but the largest modes provide only one realization.

## Voice And Pacing

- Preserve the deliberate Planck-unit prelude; it establishes the hierarchy needed by the cosmological argument.
- Let questions act as conceptual hinges rather than detached appendices.
- Keep the oscillator derivation long enough for freeze-out to feel mechanical rather than magical.
- Move from comoving wave number to physical wavelength before using the phrase “horizon exit.”
- Retain the soldiers, cliff, pockets, and coin analogies because each resolves a different conceptual obstacle.
