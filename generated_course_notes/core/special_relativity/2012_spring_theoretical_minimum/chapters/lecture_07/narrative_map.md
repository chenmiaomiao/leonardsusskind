## Pacing Rules

- Preserve the delayed arrival at gauge invariance. The charged-particle recap is the route to the concept, not detachable review.
- Keep the index recap substantial enough that scalar construction and the gauge gradient are earned.
- Do not flatten the four principles into a list; retain their physical motivations and limitations.
- Keep the complete Euler--Lagrange chain, including the missing-term correction and free-versus-dummy-index explanation.
- Present the scalar shift by x where the audience introduces it. It anticipates the endpoint proof and must not be moved after gauge invariance.
- Preserve the two gauge proofs separately: endpoint invariance of the action and commuting-derivative invariance of the field tensor.
- End with interpretation and questions, not merely the algebraic cancellation.

## Voice

The chapter should read as lecture notes prepared before the class: direct, mathematically serious, and willing to pause for a physical analogy or live correction. Humor is retained only when it clarifies teaching philosophy, terminology, or the division between principle and calculation.
