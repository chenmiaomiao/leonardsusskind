1. Answer a carried-over Doppler question with a spacetime diagram, briefly pause for course planning, and then complete the Lorentz-geometry derivation.
2. Distinguish classical particles from classical fields and use two coupled coordinates to show why one mixed action term produces reciprocal influence.
3. Couple a prescribed scalar field to a relativistic particle and recover ordinary potential energy in the slow-particle limit.
4. Vary the field as well as the worldline, rewrite the interaction with a Dirac delta, and derive sourced wave and Poisson equations.
5. Replace the static source by a moving one and connect time-dependent sources with radiation.
6. Introduce four-vectors, the Minkowski metric, index lowering, Einstein summation, contractions, scalar gradients, and invariant actions.
7. Recover Galilean relativity as the infinite-light-speed limit.
8. Add a quadratic scalar potential, derive and solve the Klein--Gordon equation, and return to the particle coupling as a toy model of a scalar-induced mass shift.
