## Reconstruction Rules

- Keep c equal to one except where the lecture restores units for a limiting argument.
- Keep the mostly-plus metric throughout the index and Klein--Gordon sections.
- Use g explicitly in both the particle potential and field source; its sign convention must remain consistent.
- Treat partial-mu phi as a covector and raise its index only with the metric.
- Standardize the scalar mass parameter to mu while showing how it becomes the particle mass after restoring Planck's constant.
- Preserve screenshots beside clean equations; do not replace source evidence with decorative redraws.
