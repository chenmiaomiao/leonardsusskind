## Reconstruction Rules

- Keep the lecture's signature with one positive time term and three negative spatial terms.
- Use c=1 for the Lorentz transformations and particle calculation, then restore c where the lecture does so explicitly.
- Use L for particle mechanics and calligraphic L for the field density.
- Treat partial_mu phi as a covector and contract it with the Minkowski metric.
- Derive the final particle-force sign from the action. The late board line is evidence of the calculation in progress, not an independent sign convention.
- Preserve the distinction between a static field, partial phi over partial t equal to zero, and the total derivative of phi along x(t).
