## Pacing Rules

- Keep Einstein's physical puzzle ahead of the tensor calculation; the calculation answers that puzzle.
- Do not compress the component matrices or the one-component boost into a slogan about field mixing.
- Preserve the distinction between identities following from the potential and dynamical equations requiring an action.
- Let the box and window constructions earn the four-current rather than introducing it as a definition without geometry.
- Keep the teaching and black-hole exchanges where they occur. They are substantial classroom material, not disposable filler.
- State the board-sign correction transparently and use one convention throughout the derivations.
- Preserve the delayed arrival at the Bianchi identity and stop before the sourced action derivation.
- End with duality and monopoles, because those questions define the frontier reached by the class.

## Voice

The chapter should read as notes prepared for the lecture: direct, explanatory, and mathematically explicit. It may retain the first-person plural used in a classroom derivation, but it must not claim that Leonard Susskind authored the reconstructed prose. Live corrections should remain visible when they illuminate the physics.
