# Lecture 10 Narrative Map

1. 00:00:08--00:09:28: posted preliminaries, Byron's convention check, Maxwell and course electric-field definitions, Franklin source signs, the transformed Maxwell equations, and the practical demand for consistency.
2. 00:09:28--00:16:03: mechanical momentum, canonical momentum, their agreement for a simple particle, and their separation in a magnetic vector potential.
3. 00:16:03--00:20:21: translation invariance, infinitesimal coordinate changes, and Noether momentum built from canonical momenta.
4. 00:20:52--00:26:52: time-translation invariance, the Hamiltonian Legendre transform, the particle derivation, kinetic versus potential energy, and the limits of the sign-flip shortcut.
5. 00:26:52--00:33:56: choosing one frame, replacing discrete coordinates by spatially labeled field values, finite differences, the x-and-t notation question, independent field values, and the first continuity challenge.
6. 00:33:56--00:43:57: action as a time integral, Lagrangian density as a spatial sum, locality, field-space velocity, canonical field momentum, and Hamiltonian density.
7. 00:43:57--00:50:49: scalar Lagrangian, Pi equals phi dot, positive field Hamiltonian, the live correction from V of x to V of phi, bounded potentials, stability, and the vacuum.
8. 00:50:49--00:55:07: why a local derivative selects one field velocity and why finite gradient energy forces continuum smoothness.
9. 00:55:07--01:00:03: T00, energy as P0, density as a current component, local conservation, the Alpha Centauri example, and integration of density to total energy.
10. 01:00:03--01:08:45: translated field profiles, delta phi, removal of the symmetry parameter, distinction from dx, Noether momentum density, and T0m.
11. 01:08:45--01:14:41: momentum of complete matter--field systems, light recoil, laser pressure, field equations as Newton laws, experiment-first and symmetry-first construction, the Higgs question, and the rope example.
12. 01:14:41--01:22:18: energy--momentum tensor preview, its future gravitational role, gauge transformation, time integration of the gauge function, and temporal gauge.
13. 01:22:18--01:30:14: explicit switch to the Maxwell electric convention, E and B from A, Maxwell Lagrangian, three spatial potential fields, and canonical momenta equal to minus E.
14. 01:30:14--01:33:17: electromagnetic Hamiltonian, positive E-squared plus B-squared energy, c equals one, and equal electric and magnetic shares in a plane wave.
15. 01:33:17--01:40:18: Noether momentum for three vector-potential fields, index assignment, visible sign uncertainty, insertion of an antisymmetric derivative, integration by parts, and the source-free Gauss constraint.
16. 01:40:18--01:44:14: E cross B as momentum density, static crossed fields, the charge-density caveat, Poynting's name, and the fixed momentum direction of an oscillating plane wave.
17. 01:44:14--01:48:20: the complete potential-to-field dictionary, temporal-gauge simplification, whether A is more fundamental, gauge observability, force, and static field momentum.
18. 01:48:20--01:54:34: density and flux rows of the energy--momentum tensor, tensor symmetry, Poynting vector as both energy flux and momentum density, and the bridge to Einstein's field equation.

## Pacing Rules

- Keep the convention repair before every later electromagnetic sign.
- Distinguish all three momenta before introducing the Hamiltonian.
- Let the lattice picture answer what the continuous spatial label means.
- Preserve the two-stage smoothness discussion: the question first, then the gradient-energy answer.
- Derive field energy and scalar momentum before applying the same mechanics to electromagnetism.
- Keep the live uncertainty about overall momentum signs, then resolve it with the gauge-invariant E cross B result.
- Retain the static crossed-field caveat and the full matter--field momentum budget.
- End with the tensor interpretation because it is the conceptual handoff to General Relativity.
