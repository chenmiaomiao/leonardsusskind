# Lecture 9 Visual Audit

## Method

- Sampled the full 5991.581-second video at broad intervals.
- Compared three neighboring accurate post-seek decodes around each retained transition.
- Inspected two broad contact sheets, two targeted contact sheets, two neighboring-frame sheets, and the final selection sheet.
- Retained original 1280 by 720 frames without crop, redraw, or synthetic replacement.

## Acceptance Rules

- The adjacent transcript topic and board content must match.
- Equations must be substantially complete.
- The lecturer may appear only when the relevant writing remains readable.
- A frame must add distinct evidence rather than duplicate a nearby state.
- Live wrong work may be kept only when the chapter identifies and resolves it.

## Rejections

- Stanford opening card and campus material.
- Audience-only views and blank boards.
- Equations while still being written when a completed neighbor existed.
- Frames with heavy body occlusion.
- Repeated states that added no new equation or diagram.
- Five inherited extraction windows that omitted the entire wave derivation and most of the action calculation.

## Result

Twenty-four frames remain. They cover the plane-wave ansatz and geometry, scalar and electromagnetic Lagrangians, component variation, source coupling, integration by parts, and the live continuity-sign correction. Every retained image is cited once in the chapter and has a timestamp, rationale, and caption record in metadata.json.
