## Pacing Rules

- Finish the plane-wave calculation and its questions before introducing the action.
- Keep the scalar rehearsal because it prepares the vector variation.
- Do not replace the representative component derivative with a one-line covariant result.
- Let local current conservation emerge from the box picture before using it in the gauge proof.
- Preserve both the wrong-sign board and the correction; the correction is part of the reasoning.
- Leave historical gauge terminology and duality after the mathematical climax.

## Voice
