\chapter*{About These Notes}
These independently edited companion notes follow the 2013 Statistical Mechanics lectures by Leonard Susskind. They preserve the lectures' explanatory order, substantive classroom exchanges, mathematical derivations, and selected blackboard evidence. They are not an original manuscript by Professor Susskind and have not been reviewed or endorsed by him.

The edition was prepared by \href{https://lazying.art}{LazyingArt LLC} with the open-source \href{https://github.com/lachlanchen/Video2Book}{Video2Book} workflow. Complete machine transcripts and subtitle files remain available separately in the repository and may contain recognition errors.
