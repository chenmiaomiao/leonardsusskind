## Equation Reading

- Figures 2 and 3 support the two sound estimates,
  \(3k_B T/m\) and \((1/m)(\partial P/\partial\rho)\), plus the room-air numerology.
- Figures 4 through 6 preserve the classical sequence
  \(H=p^2/(2m)+\kappa x^2/2\),
  \(Z\propto(\beta\omega)^{-1}\), and
  \(\langle H\rangle=1/\beta=T\).
- Figures 7 through 9 preserve the quantum sequence
  \(Z=(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})^{-1}\),
  \(E_{\rm exc}=\hbar\omega/(e^{\beta\hbar\omega}-1)\), and its low-temperature limit.
- The board omits the classical \(1/h\) phase-space normalization and initially shifts away the oscillator zero-point energy. Both conventions are explained in the typeset derivation.

## Diagram Reading

- The string sketch is a qualitative normal-mode picture, not a quantitative waveform plot.
- The phase-space drawings are schematic two-dimensional slices of a much higher-dimensional space.
- In the coarse-graining drawing, black lines represent the exact volume-preserving filament and red loops represent finite-resolution cells. Their overlap belongs to the description, not the exact Hamiltonian map.
- The bounded-region and filament drawings motivate equilibration but do not by themselves prove ergodicity.
- The double pendulum is an example of sensitive dependence without friction; the drawing is not a phase portrait.
