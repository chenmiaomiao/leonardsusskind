# Chapter Plan

## Lecture Arc

The chapter has two connected halves. The first tests the second law against
Poincare recurrence. A gas spreads out rapidly, yet a finite
measure-preserving system must eventually revisit rare macrostates. The
high-dimensional volume ratio explains the huge waiting time and leads into
the deeper question of why the universe began at low entropy. The discussion
then follows the lecture through Boltzmann brains, coherent history, and life
as a stationary structure sustained by energy flow.

The second half turns to magnetic order. A qualitative discussion of domains
and spontaneous symmetry breaking motivates a solvable model of independent
two-state spins in an external field. Combinatorics gives the partition
function, differentiation gives the magnetization, and the absence of a phase
transition motivates the interacting zero-field Ising chain.

## Mathematical Decisions

- State recurrence for a finite-measure, measure-preserving flow as return
  arbitrarily close to the initial state, not exact equality of all real
  coordinates.
- Use
  \(P(A)=\exp(S_A-S_{\rm eq})=e^{-\Delta S}\), rather than treating
  \(e^{-S_{\rm eq}}\) as a generally normalized formula.
- Restore the microscopic correlation scale:
  \(\tau_{\rm rec}\sim\tau_{\rm mix}/P(A)\).
- Preserve the lecture's rough \(10^{30}\) particle rhetoric while noting
  that an ordinary room is closer to \(10^{27}\); either exponent makes the
  same physical point.
- Distinguish configuration magnetization
  \(M_{\rm conf}=(n-m)/N\) from its ensemble average
  \(M=\langle M_{\rm conf}\rangle\).
- Keep the lecture's sign convention
  \(E=(n-m)\mu H\), which implies
  \(M=-\tanh(\beta\mu H)\) for positive \(H\).
- Use
  \(Z_N=[2\cosh(\beta\mu H)]^N\) and
  \(\langle E\rangle=-\partial_\beta\log Z_N\).
- Define the ferromagnetic Ising chain by
  \({\cal H}=-J\sum_i\sigma_i\sigma_{i+1}\), \(J>0\).
- Explain spontaneous symmetry breaking with the thermodynamic limit taken
  before the selecting field is removed.

## Caution Notes

- The source's \(e^{-S}\) and room-particle estimates are heuristic and need
  the local qualifications above.
- The cosmological discussion is a statistical diagnostic, not a complete
  cosmological measure theory.
- The in-class magnetization definition is corrected immediately; retain only
  the corrected form.
- The transcript corrupts sinh and tanh phonetically; use standard notation.
- The classroom history of Ising is inaccurate. Preserve the narrative pivot
  but correct the record: Ising solved the one-dimensional case and found no
  finite-temperature transition; Kramers--Wannier and Onsager established the
  key two-dimensional results.
- At finite size and exactly zero field the symmetric ensemble has zero mean
  magnetization. Do not present direction selection without the relevant
  order of limits.
