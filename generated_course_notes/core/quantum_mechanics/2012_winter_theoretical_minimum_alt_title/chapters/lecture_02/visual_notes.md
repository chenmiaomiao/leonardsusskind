## Frame Selection

- Prefer a completed board state over the first instant at which a symbol appears.
- Keep the lecturer outside the mathematical object whenever a nearby timestamp permits it.

- Do not infer hidden marks outside the visible crop; use transcript evidence for any cleaned notation.
