# Mathematical Inventory

- Common eigenvectors: $L|i\rangle=l_i|i\rangle$ and $M|i\rangle=m_i|i\rangle$.
- Simultaneous measurability: $[L,M]=0$.
- Unitary evolution: $|\psi(t)\rangle=U(t)|\psi(0)\rangle$ and $U^\dagger U=I$.
- Infinitesimal generator: $U(\epsilon)=I-i\epsilon H+O(\epsilon^2)$ and $H^\dagger=H$.
- State equation: $d|\psi\rangle/dt=-iH|\psi\rangle$.
- Energy equation: $H|E_i\rangle=E_i|E_i\rangle$.
- Average motion: $d\langle L\rangle/dt=-i\langle[L,H]\rangle$.
- Product rule: $[AB,C]=A[B,C]+[A,C]B$.
- Classical correspondence: $\{A,B\}\leftrightarrow[A,B]/(i\hbar)$.
- Energy conservation: $d\langle H\rangle/dt=0$ for time-independent $H$.
- Energy phases: $\alpha_j(t)=\alpha_j(0)e^{-iE_jt}$.
- Matrix frequencies: $\langle L\rangle(t)=\sum_{k,j}\alpha_k^*(0)\alpha_j(0)e^{i(E_k-E_j)t}L_{kj}$.
- Pauli algebra: $[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k$.
- Field along $z$: $H=\omega\sigma_z/2$.
- Precession: $\dot{\langle\bm\sigma\rangle}=\omega\bm n\times\langle\bm\sigma\rangle$.
- General spin Hamiltonian: $H=(\omega/2)\bm\sigma\cdot\bm n$.
- Spectrum: $\operatorname{spec}(\bm\sigma\cdot\bm n)=\{+1,-1\}$ and $E_\pm=\pm\omega/2$.
