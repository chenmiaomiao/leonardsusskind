# Lecture 7 Editorial Analysis

## Source Arc

The lecture postpones Bell's theorem until entanglement has a precise definition. It moves from wave functions as basis coordinates to expectation values, local tensor-factor observables, product-state factorization, and correlations. The singlet then demonstrates a pure whole with maximally mixed parts. Reduced density matrices provide the local description; measurement becomes system-apparatus entanglement; the observer boundary is allowed to move; and no signaling is separated from Bell's failure of separated classical simulation.

## Physics Resolutions

- More than one nonzero reduced eigenvalue, not every eigenvalue, diagnoses entanglement for a global pure state.
- Local mixedness certifies entanglement only when the global state is known to be pure.
- The singlet is lower for antiferromagnetic coupling \(J>0\); the ordering reverses with the sign.
- The expectation of \(\boldsymbol{\sigma}\cdot\boldsymbol{\tau}\) may be reconstructed on subensembles, while a one-pair singlet-triplet measurement requires a joint apparatus.
- Bob-local trace-preserving operations leave Alice's unconditioned state unchanged; outcome-conditioned states require communication.
- Bell excludes local hidden-variable models under its assumptions but does not create a superluminal signaling channel.
