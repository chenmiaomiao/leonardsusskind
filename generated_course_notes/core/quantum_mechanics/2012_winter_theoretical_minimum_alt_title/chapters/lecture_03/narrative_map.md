# Narrative Map

## Sequence

### Notation Before Physics

Components are projections onto an orthonormal basis. The completeness relation rebuilds either a ket or a bra and becomes the basic algebraic move used throughout the chapter.

### Operators Become Matrices

Linearity defines the machine. Projecting \(M|A\rangle=|B\rangle\) and inserting a complete basis yields \(\sum_jm_{ij}\alpha_j=\beta_i\), the row-by-column matrix rule.

### Conjugation Becomes a Dagger

Bracket-independent bra action leads to \(M^\dagger\). Its matrix is obtained by transposition and complex conjugation. Operators equal to their dagger are Hermitian.

### Spectra Become Measurement Language

Hermitian eigenvalues are real, unequal-eigenvalue eigenvectors are orthogonal, and the full eigenbasis is complete. These facts become possible outcomes, definite-value states, and distinguishability.

### Probabilities and Preparation

Projection supplies a complex amplitude; its squared magnitude supplies probability. Completeness gives unit normalization, common phase is unobservable, and a recorded result prepares the corresponding eigenstate.

### The Pauli Matrices

The known up/down, right/left, and in/out states determine the matrices for the three spin components. Their basis-dependent entries reproduce the basis-independent analyzer experiments.

## Recurring Distinctions

- Abstract operator versus basis-dependent matrix.
- Linear map versus anti-linear conjugation.
- Probability amplitude versus probability.
- Eigenvector representative versus eigendirection.
- Outcome probability versus signed ensemble average.
- Kinematics and measurement versus time evolution.
