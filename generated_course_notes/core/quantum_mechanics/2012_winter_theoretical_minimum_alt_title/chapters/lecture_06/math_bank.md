# Mathematical Inventory

- Spin Hamiltonian: \(H=\omega\sigma_z/2\), with \(E_\pm=\pm\omega/2\) and gap \(\Delta E=\omega\) for \(\hbar=1\).
- Superposed energy state: \(\ket\psi=\alpha\ket u+\beta\ket d\).
- Photon statistics: \(P(1\text{ photon})=|\alpha|^2\), \(P(0)=|\beta|^2\), and \(\langle E_{\rm emitted}\rangle=|\alpha|^2\omega\).
- Lifetime linewidth: \(\Delta E\sim\hbar/\tau\).
- Measurement expansion: \(\ket\psi=\sum_i\alpha_i\ket{\lambda_i}\), with outcome probability \(|\alpha_i|^2\).
- Composite space: \(\mathcal S_{AI}=\mathcal S_A\otimes\mathcal S_I\).
- Dimension: \(\dim\mathcal S_{AI}=D_A D_I\).
- Inner product: \(\langle b,j|a,i\rangle=\delta_{ba}\delta_{ji}\).
- General composite state: \(\ket\Psi=\sum_{a,i}c_{ai}\ket{a,i}\).
- Two-spin basis: \(\ket{uu},\ket{ud},\ket{du},\ket{dd}\).
- Product expansion: \((\alpha_u\ket u+\alpha_d\ket d)\otimes(\beta_u\ket u+\beta_d\ket d)\).
- Rank-one condition: \(c_{uu}c_{dd}=c_{ud}c_{du}\).
- Local embeddings: \(\sigma_m\equiv\sigma_m\otimes I\) and \(\tau_n\equiv I\otimes\sigma_n\).
- Pauli action: \(\sigma_x\ket u=\ket d\), \(\sigma_z\ket u=\ket u\), and \(\sigma_y\ket u=i\ket d\), with the corresponding down-state rules.
- Local commutation: \([\sigma_m,\tau_n]=0\).
- Singlet: \(\ket S=(\ket{ud}-\ket{du})/\sqrt2\).
- Pure one-spin Bloch vector: \(|\bm r|=1\), where \(r_i=\langle\sigma_i\rangle\).
- Singlet local averages: \(\langle\sigma_i\rangle_S=\langle\tau_i\rangle_S=0\).
- Antiferromagnetic coupling: \(H_{\rm int}=J\bm\sigma\cdot\bm\tau\), with singlet eigenvalue \(-3J\) and triplet eigenvalue \(+J\).
- Reduced state and entropy: \(\rho_\sigma=I/2\) and \(S(\rho_\sigma)=\log2\).
- Singlet basis form: \(\ket S=(\ket{+_{\bm n},-_{\bm n}}-\ket{-_{\bm n},+_{\bm n}})/\sqrt2\).
- Correlation tensor: \(\langle\sigma_m\tau_n\rangle_S=-\delta_{mn}\).
- Directional correlation: \(\langle(\bm\sigma\cdot\bm a)(\bm\tau\cdot\bm b)\rangle_S=-\bm a\cdot\bm b\).
