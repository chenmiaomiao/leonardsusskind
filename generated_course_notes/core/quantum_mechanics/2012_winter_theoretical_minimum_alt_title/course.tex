\chapter*{About These Notes}
These independently edited companion notes follow lectures by Leonard
Susskind. They were reconstructed from machine transcripts, subtitles,
and selected blackboard frames, then checked against the available source
material. They are not an original manuscript by Professor Susskind and
have not been reviewed or endorsed by him.

The edition was prepared by \href{https://lazying.art}{LazyingArt LLC}
using the open-source
\href{https://github.com/lachlanchen/Video2Book}{Video2Book} workflow.
Machine transcripts remain available separately and may contain
recognition errors. Editorial clarifications and nontrivial
reconstructions are identified in footnotes.
