# Figure Descriptions

## lecture_01_figure_01.png

At 00:23:55 the left side of the board shows the barrier and two slits. The
right side shows the screen axis and a completed oscillating arrival profile.
The profile repeatedly returns to the axis, displaying the destructive
interference nodes discussed in the lecture.

## lecture_01_figure_02.png

At 00:28:45 three labeled points form a triangle: H at lower left, T at the
top, and F at lower right. Directed arrows run H to T, T to F, and F to H,
giving the deterministic three-state cycle.

## lecture_01_figure_03.png

At 00:46:30 the board contains two broad, nearly coincident momentum curves
and a horizontal arrow showing their relative displacement. These are the
initial plate-momentum distribution and the same distribution after recoil.
Older drawings above and to the left are not used.

## lecture_01_figure_04.png

At 01:03:18 the board shows \(E=hf=\hbar\omega\), \(\omega=2\pi f\),
\(p=h/\lambda\), \(c/\lambda=f\), and \(\lambda<\Delta x\). This is the
clearest single frame of the microscope derivation.

## lecture_01_figure_05.png

At 01:04:45 a wavy incoming photon strikes a localized dot and several arrows
leave in possible scattering directions. The board retains
\(E=hf=\hbar\omega\) and the resolution condition.

## lecture_01_figure_06.png

At 01:13:15 the first reading is written as \(x\pm\lambda\), the later reading
as \(x\pm\lambda+vt\), and the board gives a velocity uncertainty of order
\(\lambda/t\) beside \(d/t\).

## lecture_01_figure_07.png

At 01:32:35 the board shows ket-vector addition and the linear combination
\(\alpha\ket a+\beta\ket b\), emphasizing closure in the vector space.

## lecture_01_figure_08.png

At 01:36:45 the lower board writes a complex function as a real part plus
\(i\) times an imaginary part and then adds two functions. The earlier ket
relations remain visible above.

## lecture_01_figure_09.png

At 01:42:00 the board gives complete four-component columns for
componentwise addition and multiplication by one scalar \(\alpha\).

## lecture_01_figure_10.png

At 01:49:30 the unobstructed board reads
\(zz^\ast=(x+iy)(x-iy)=x^2+y^2\), the squared complex magnitude.
