# Mathematics Bank

## Classical And Quantum Probability

- Classical two-route expectation:
  \(P_{\mathrm{cl}}(y)=P_1(y)+P_2(y)\).
- Observed destructive-interference nodes:
  \(P_{12}(y_0)=0\) while \(P_1(y_0),P_2(y_0)>0\).
- Three-state reversible cycle:
  \(H\mapsto T\mapsto F\mapsto H\).

## Photon And Microscope Relations

- \(E=hf=\hbar\omega\), with
  \(\omega=2\pi f\) and \(\hbar=h/(2\pi)\).
- \(E=cp\), hence \(p=E/c\), for light.
- \(T=1/f\) and \(c=\lambda f\).
- \(p_\gamma=h/\lambda\).
- Imaging scale: \(\lambda\lesssim\Delta x\).
- Momentum kick:
  \(\Delta p_{\mathrm{kick}}\sim h/\lambda\).
- Heuristic product:
  \(\Delta x\,\Delta p_{\mathrm{kick}}\gtrsim h\), explicitly marked as
  order-of-magnitude rather than the sharp operator inequality.

## Gentle Velocity Measurement

- \(x_1=x+O(\lambda)\).
- \(x_2=x+vt+O(\lambda)\).
- \(d=x_2-x_1=vt+O(\lambda)\).
- \(v_{\mathrm{meas}}=d/t=v+O(\lambda/t)\).

## State Spaces

- Classical phase-space point: \((x,p)\).
- Classical incomplete knowledge:
  \(\rho(x,p)\geq0\) and \(\int dx\,dp\,\rho=1\).
- Complex linear closure:
  \(\alpha\ket a+\beta\ket b\in V\).
- Function operations:
  \((\psi+\phi)(x)=\psi(x)+\phi(x)\) and
  \((\alpha\psi)(x)=\alpha\psi(x)\).
- Fixed-length columns add and scale componentwise.
- \(z=x+iy\), \(z^\ast=x-iy\), and \(z^\ast z=x^2+y^2=|z|^2\).
