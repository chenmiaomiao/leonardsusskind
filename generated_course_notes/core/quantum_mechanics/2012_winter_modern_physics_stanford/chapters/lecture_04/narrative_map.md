# Narrative Map

## Opening

The discrete rules are not discarded when position becomes continuous.
Their notation changes in a controlled way: sums become integrals,
probabilities become densities, and Kronecker deltas become Dirac deltas.

## Sequence

1. Review discrete probability, orthonormality, and Born amplitudes.
2. Replace point probabilities by interval probabilities and a density.
3. Define the Dirac delta by its sampling action.
4. Derive position amplitudes and the continuous Born rule.
5. Use a lattice spacing to derive delta normalization.
6. Cut a circle into an interval and impose periodic boundary conditions.
7. Solve and normalize the momentum eigenfunctions.
8. Use periodicity to quantize momentum and orbital angular momentum.
9. Separate the infinite-radius continuum limit from classical mechanics.
10. Define compatibility through common eigenvectors and commutators.
11. Derive the canonical position-momentum commutator.
12. Add two path amplitudes and expose the interference cross term.
13. Relate path geometry to fringe spacing and phase gradients.
14. Preserve the particle-by-particle, photon, aperture, and visibility
    questions.
15. Defer which-path entanglement and reversibility until composite systems
    and Hamiltonian evolution are available.

## Voice

The chapter reads as a prepared derivation rather than a report. Classroom
questions remain explicit where they expose a misconception or motivate a
technical refinement. Standard mathematics is added only where it completes
or corrects the argument without changing its chronology.

## Ending

The final paragraph does not summarize the chapter. It identifies the exact
next requirement: a Hamiltonian and a law of time evolution.
