# Mathematical Bank

## Continuum Basis

- Discrete normalization: sum over n of P(n) equals one.
- Continuous normalization: integral of rho(x) dx equals one.
- Delta sampling: integral of delta(x-y) f(x) dx equals f(y).
- Position basis: inner product of x and y equals delta(x-y).
- Completeness: integral dx |x><x| equals the identity.
- Born density: rho(y) equals the squared magnitude of psi(y).
- Lattice regulator: |x_n> equals |n> divided by sqrt(a), with overlap
  delta_nm divided by a.

## Particle on a Circle

- Circumference: L equals 2 pi R.
- Boundary condition: psi(x+2 pi R) equals psi(x).
- Momentum: p-hat equals minus i hbar d/dx.
- Normalized eigenfunction: psi_n equals exp(i n x/R) divided by
  sqrt(2 pi R).
- Momentum spectrum: p_n equals n hbar/R.
- Orbital angular momentum: L_n equals n hbar.
- Momentum spacing: Delta p equals hbar/R.

## Compatibility

- Commutator: [A,B] equals AB minus BA.
- Canonical relation: [x,p] equals i hbar times the identity.
- Common eigenvectors are impossible because the canonical commutator does
  not annihilate any nonzero vector.

## Interference

- Added amplitudes: psi equals psi_1 plus psi_2.
- Local equal-amplitude density: 2 plus 2 cos((p-q)y/hbar).
- General density: rho_1 squared plus rho_2 squared plus
  2 rho_1 rho_2 cos(Delta phi).
- Far-field fringe spacing: Delta y approximately lambda ell/d.
- Local phase gradient: p_y equals hbar d phi/dy.
- Elastic deflection: p_x squared plus p_y squared equals p squared.
- de Broglie wavelength: lambda equals h/p.
