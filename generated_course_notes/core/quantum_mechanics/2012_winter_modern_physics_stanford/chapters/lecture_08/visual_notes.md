# Visual Evidence

## Selection Method

Candidate frames were sampled around transcript timestamps and compared in
adjacent-frame contact sheets. A frame was retained only when the relevant
board content was complete, legible relative to nearby alternatives, and
matched the surrounding derivation.

## Limitations

The source video is 320 by 240 pixels. Frames are preserved as direct board
evidence, while all equations are reconstructed and checked independently.
Lecturer-obscured and partially erased candidates were rejected whenever a
clearer nearby frame existed.
