# Visual Evidence

## Selection Method

Candidate frames were sampled around transcript timestamps and inspected in
adjacent-frame contact sheets. A frame was accepted only when its board
content matched the neighboring argument and was more legible than nearby
alternatives.

## Limitations

The source is 320 by 240 pixels. Frames are retained as historical board
evidence, while all equations are separately reconstructed and checked.
Lecturer-obscured candidates were rejected whenever a clearer nearby frame
existed.
