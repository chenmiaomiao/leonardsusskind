# Editorial Analysis

## Lecture Arc

The lecture completes the circular-polarization observable, then steps back to
identify the two operational uses of a quantum state: expectation values and
transition probabilities. Those uses motivate the removal of global phase.
The resulting two-parameter state is interpreted as a polarization ellipse,
and analyzer probabilities recover its orientation and handedness.

The second half turns from states to motion. Reversible classical evolution
preserves distinctions; closed quantum evolution preserves the richer
inner-product relation. Operators on bras, adjoints, Hermitian observables,
and unitary transformations are introduced in that order.
