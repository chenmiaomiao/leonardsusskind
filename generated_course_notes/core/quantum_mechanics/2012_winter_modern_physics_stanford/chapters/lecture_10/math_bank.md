# Mathematical Bank

## Classical Oscillator

\[
L=\frac12m\dot q^2-\frac12m\omega^2q^2,
\qquad
x=\sqrt m\,q,
\qquad
\ddot x=-\omega^2x.
\]

\[
H=\frac12p^2+\frac12\omega^2x^2.
\]

## Schrodinger Problem

\[
\hat p=-i\hbar\partial_x,
\qquad
\hat H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2}\partial_x^2+\frac12\omega^2x^2.
\]

\[
i\hbar\partial_t\psi=\hat H\psi,
\qquad
\hat H\psi_E=E\psi_E.
\]

## Ground State

\[
\psi_0(x)=
\left(\frac{\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}
\exp\left(-\frac{\omega x^2}{2\hbar}\right),
\qquad
E_0=\frac12\hbar\omega.
\]

\[
(\Delta x)^2=\frac{\hbar}{2\omega}.
\]

## Ladder Algebra

\[
a=\frac{p-i\omega x}{\sqrt{2\hbar\omega}},
\qquad
a^\dagger=\frac{p+i\omega x}{\sqrt{2\hbar\omega}},
\qquad
[a,a^\dagger]=1.
\]

\[
N=a^\dagger a,
\qquad
H=\hbar\omega\left(N+\frac12\right).
\]

\[
|n\rangle=\frac{(a^\dagger)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}|0\rangle,
\quad
a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle,
\quad
a|n\rangle=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle.
\]

\[
E_n=\hbar\omega\left(n+\frac12\right).
\]

## Position-Space Eigenfunctions

\[
\psi_n(x)=
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2^n n!}}
\left(\frac{\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}
H_n\left(\sqrt{\frac{\omega}{\hbar}}x\right)
e^{-\omega x^2/(2\hbar)}.
\]
