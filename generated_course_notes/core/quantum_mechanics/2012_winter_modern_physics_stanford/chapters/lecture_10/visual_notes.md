# Visual Review

Twenty-five source frames are used in chronological order. Selection favored
complete board states with the relevant equation or diagram visible over
nearby occluded, transitional, or partially erased frames. Every timestamp is
inside its figure environment, and every caption is limited to visible
evidence plus the immediately corresponding derivation.

The compiled chapter was reviewed as a 23-page contact sheet. Equations and
figures remain legible, no section heading is stranded, and the final
discussion closes without an orphaned line or empty page. No campus opening,
institutional title, empty board, duplicate, or closing credit frame is used.
