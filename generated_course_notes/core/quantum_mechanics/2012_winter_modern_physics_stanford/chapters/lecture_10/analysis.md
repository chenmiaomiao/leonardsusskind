# Lecture 10 Analysis

## Central Arc

The final lecture of this quantum-mechanics sequence develops the harmonic
oscillator from its classical origin to its complete quantum spectrum. Its
mathematical spine is:

1. expand a stable potential about equilibrium;
2. quantize the oscillator Hamiltonian;
3. use normalizability to explain discrete bound-state energies;
4. derive the Gaussian ground state and zero-point energy;
5. factor the Hamiltonian into ladder operators;
6. construct the complete energy ladder and its Hermite wave functions.

## Source Repair

The board discussion reverses operator names while checking the commutator.
The final chapter uses one consistent convention throughout:

\[
a=\frac{p-i\omega x}{\sqrt{2\hbar\omega}},
\qquad
a^\dagger=\frac{p+i\omega x}{\sqrt{2\hbar\omega}},
\qquad
[a,a^\dagger]=1.
\]

This convention makes the first operator annihilate the Gaussian and gives
\(H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+1/2)\).
