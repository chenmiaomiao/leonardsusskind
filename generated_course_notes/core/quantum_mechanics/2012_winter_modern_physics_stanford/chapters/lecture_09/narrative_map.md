# Narrative Map

## 00:00:13--00:18:19: Why a New Mechanics Was Needed

Mechanistic repair, blackbody radiation, Planck's rule, Einstein's photons,
Compton scattering, old quantum orbits, spontaneous probability, and
Einstein's later objections.

## 00:18:20--00:28:16: Quantum-First Free Motion

The abstract Schrodinger equation, momentum as the generator of translation,
the even free Hamiltonian, position probability, and the free-particle
differential equation.

## 00:28:17--00:50:51: Plane Waves and Momentum Space

The plane-wave ansatz, sign convention, energy degeneracy under momentum
reversal, general Fourier superposition, momentum probability, energy
probability, admissibility, separability, and momentum-space phase.

## 00:50:52--01:00:55: Initial-Value Evolution

Forward and inverse Fourier transforms, momentum-space phase evolution,
packet spreading, phase and group velocity, and the nonapplicability of the
nonrelativistic equation to photons.

## 01:00:56--01:34:48: The Algebraic Correspondence

Expectation-value evolution, the classical Poisson law, derivative
identities, the canonical commutator, antisymmetry, product rules, and
operator-ordering limitations.

## 01:34:49--01:47:35: Potential Motion and the Classical Limit

The Schrodinger equation with a potential, exact motion of mean position and
mean momentum, the average force, and the controlled recovery of Newtonian
motion.
