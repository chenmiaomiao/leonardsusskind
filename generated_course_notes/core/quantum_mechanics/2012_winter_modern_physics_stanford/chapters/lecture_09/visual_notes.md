# Visual Review

Twenty-two source frames are used in chronological order. Selection favored
complete board states over close-ups that omitted the surrounding
derivation. Every figure caption states only what is visibly supported, and
each timestamp appears inside its figure environment.

The compiled chapter was reviewed as a 23-page contact sheet. Figure sizes
remain legible without dominating the text, no section heading is stranded,
and the final Ehrenfest derivation remains continuous across the last
mathematical spread. No opening campus image, empty board, closing credit, or
unrelated frame is included.
