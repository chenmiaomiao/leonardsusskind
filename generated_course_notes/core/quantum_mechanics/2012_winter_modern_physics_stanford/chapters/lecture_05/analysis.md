# Editorial Analysis

## Lecture Shape

The lecture has two linked halves. The first derives continuum momentum
normalization, Fourier duality, operator actions, and the wave-packet bridge
to classical mechanics. The second realizes the same logic in the
two-dimensional polarization space of a photon.

## Editorial Decisions

- The exact lattice-spacing rescaling replaces the verbally tangled
  Kronecker-to-Dirac discussion.
- Wave number k and physical momentum p are separated by p = hbar k.
- The classical bridge is identified as an announced Ehrenfest result, not
  treated as already proved.
- The wire-grid mechanism is retained before the single-photon idealization.
- States, eigenvalues, single-shot outcomes, ensemble probabilities, and
  projector expectations are kept distinct.
- Eighteen focused classroom exchanges preserve questions that advance the
  reasoning.
- Fourteen direct frames span both halves of the lecture; incomplete and
  redundant board states were rejected.

## Result

The accepted chapter follows all 44 substantive chronological movements and
compiles as a warning-free 17-page standalone PDF.
