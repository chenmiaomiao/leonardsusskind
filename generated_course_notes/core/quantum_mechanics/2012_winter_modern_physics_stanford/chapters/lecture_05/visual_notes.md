# Visual Evidence

Fourteen frames were read directly from the source video. Their timestamps
are recorded inside the corresponding figure floats in content.tex.

The first seven frames document the periodic regulator, continuum
normalization, completeness, Fourier transformation, operator duality, and
wave-packet geometry. The final seven document the electromagnetic-wave
picture, sequential polarizers, the horizontal-vertical operator, the
diagonal preparation, the rotated basis, its matrix check, and projector
probabilities.

The source is a low-resolution lecture recording. Frames are therefore used
as blackboard evidence and visual continuity, while every formula is
reconstructed in LaTeX. No decorative campus image or nonphysics frame is
retained.
