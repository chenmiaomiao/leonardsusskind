# Narrative Map

## First Half

1. Regulate the infinite line by identifying the ends of a finite interval.
2. Normalize periodic plane waves and quantize their allowed wave numbers.
3. Use orthogonality and the spacing Delta k to pass exactly from Kronecker
   to Dirac normalization.
4. Introduce dyads and completeness because they make changes of basis
   algebraic.
5. Derive the Fourier transform pair and the reciprocal operator actions.
6. Explain why position and momentum are alternative representations of one
   state, not simultaneous sharp data.
7. Preview wave packets as the eventual bridge to classical motion.

## Second Half

1. Recast incompatibility in the two-state polarization space of one photon.
2. Begin with the classical field geometry and the mechanism of a wire-grid
   polarizer.
3. Use polarizers first as preparers and then as measurement apparatuses.
4. Construct the horizontal-vertical basis and its Hermitian observable.
5. Rotate the apparatus, construct the diagonal basis, and derive equal
   Born probabilities.
6. Build the rotated observable and show that the two bases are
   incompatible.
7. End by separating eigenvalues recorded in one run from probabilities
   inferred across an ensemble.

## Voice

The accepted prose states the physics directly, keeps useful classroom
obstacles as focused exchanges, and lets the two-state example answer the
questions raised by the continuous-variable formalism.
