# Narrative Map

## Opening Method

The lecture begins by defending an Einstein-first route. The equivalence principle is not treated as a polished theorem but as a simple physical observation whose consequences force the geometry to appear. Special relativity is temporarily set aside so that the gravity-acceleration connection can be isolated without unnecessary machinery.

## Chronological Movements

1. **The elevator becomes mathematics.** Ground and elevator coordinates are related by the floor height \(L(t)\). Uniform motion establishes the inertial baseline.
2. **Acceleration manufactures a force.** Replacing \(L(t)=vt\) with \(L(t)=gt^2/2\) produces the extra term \(-mg\). A live sign check makes the direction physically transparent.
3. **The force looks gravitational.** Its proportionality to mass makes the motion independent of test-body mass, the same distinctive feature as ordinary gravity.
4. **Algebra becomes spacetime geometry.** Linear coordinate grids preserve straight lines; accelerated coordinate grids are parabolic.
5. **Light supplies the first prediction.** A horizontal inertial light ray is curved in the accelerated frame, so equivalence implies gravitational bending.
6. **Tides limit the slogan.** A radial field cannot be erased over an extended region. The falling two-thousand-mile body, polar coordinates, rockets, crystals, and the carousel distinguish free-fall deformation from support forces and coordinate artifacts.
7. **Local equivalence survives.** At a point the common acceleration can be removed; variation of the field across a finite laboratory remains.
8. **Gravity becomes a geometry problem.** The Minkowski interval leads to Euclidean and then general local distance laws. The sphere, global shortest paths, folded sheets, and tangent planes separate coordinate appearance, embedding, and intrinsic curvature.
9. **Flatness becomes a transformation question.** A metric is flat on a region only if one coordinate system reduces it to \(\delta_{mn}\) throughout that region, not merely at one point.
10. **Tensor analysis supplies the language.** Coordinate displacements motivate contravariant vectors, scalar gradients motivate covectors, and products motivate higher-rank transformation laws. The metric then returns as a rank-two covariant tensor.

## Lecture Rhythm

The chapter retains the lecture's repeated pattern: state a strong claim, invite the obvious objection, test it with a concrete body or coordinate system, and then refine the claim. The classroom questions are therefore placed where they change the argument rather than collected at the end.

The close is intentionally open. The machinery has identified what must transform, but the systematic curvature test belongs to the lectures that follow.
