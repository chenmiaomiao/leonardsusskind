# Narrative Map

## Geometry Becomes Motion

The lecture begins with the covariant derivative, turns it into parallel transport, and then applies transport to a curve's own tangent. This makes the geodesic equation feel earned rather than introduced as a gravitational law by decree.

## Space Becomes Spacetime

The parameter in the geodesic equation changes from positive-definite arc length to proper time. The Lorentzian signature, sign convention, and flat metric are established before acceleration is introduced.

## Circles Become Hyperbolae

Ordinary polar coordinates provide the rhythm of the construction. Circular functions become hyperbolic functions, angle becomes rapidity, and circles of fixed radius become uniformly accelerated hyperbolic worldlines.

## A Rigid Accelerated Elevator

The family must have position-dependent proper acceleration to preserve neighboring proper distances. Localizing near one large-radius observer produces a nearly uniform field with a time-time metric correction proportional to the Newtonian potential.

## Geodesics Become Newton's Law

In the slow-motion limit only the time-time Christoffel term survives. Its spatial derivative gives downward acceleration. The final post-lecture exchange then explains why this force can vanish in inertial coordinates: the connection is not a tensor, while curvature is.

## Looking Ahead

Replacing the uniform potential by minus GM over r suggests the characteristic Schwarzschild time coefficient and horizon scale. The lecture stops before claiming a derivation because Einstein's field equations are still missing.
