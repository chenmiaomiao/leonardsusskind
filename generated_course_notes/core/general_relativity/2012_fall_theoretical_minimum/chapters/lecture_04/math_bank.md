# Mathematical Spine

## Covariant Differentiation

\[
\nabla_rV_s=\partial_rV_s-\Gamma^p{}_{rs}V_p,
\qquad
\nabla_mV^n=\partial_mV^n+\Gamma^n{}_{mr}V^r.
\]

\[
\Gamma^p{}_{rs}
=\frac12g^{pn}
\left(
\partial_sg_{nr}+\partial_rg_{ns}-\partial_ng_{rs}
\right).
\]

## Parallel Transport and Geodesics

\[
dV^n+\Gamma^n{}_{mr}V^r\,dx^m=0.
\]

\[
t^m=\frac{dx^m}{ds},
\qquad
\frac{d^2x^n}{ds^2}
+\Gamma^n{}_{mr}
\frac{dx^m}{ds}\frac{dx^r}{ds}=0.
\]

## Lorentzian Convention

\[
d\tau^2=-g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu,
\qquad
g_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1).
\]

## Rindler Coordinates

\[
x=r\cosh\omega,
\qquad
t=r\sinh\omega,
\qquad
x^2-t^2=r^2.
\]

\[
d\tau^2=r^2d\omega^2-dr^2.
\]

\[
a=\frac1R\quad(c=1),
\qquad
a=\frac{c^2}{R}.
\]

## Local Uniform Field

\[
r=R+y,
\qquad
t=R\omega,
\qquad
R=\frac{c^2}{g}.
\]

\[
d\tau^2
=
\left(
1+\frac{2y}{R}+\frac{y^2}{R^2}
\right)dt^2-dy^2.
\]

\[
d\tau^2\simeq(1+2gy)dt^2-dy^2
\qquad(c=1).
\]

\[
\Phi=gy,
\qquad
g_{tt}=-(1+2\Phi).
\]

## Newtonian Limit

\[
\frac{d^2y}{d\tau^2}\simeq-\Gamma^y{}_{tt},
\qquad
\Gamma^y{}_{tt}=-\frac12\partial_yg_{tt}.
\]

\[
\frac{d^2y}{d\tau^2}
=\frac12\partial_yg_{tt}
=-g.
\]

\[
g_{tt}=-(1+2\Phi)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\frac{d^2x^i}{dt^2}=-\partial_i\Phi.
\]

## Schwarzschild Preview

\[
\Phi(r)=-\frac{GM}{r},
\qquad
g_{tt}\simeq-\left(1-\frac{2GM}{r}\right)
\quad(c=1).
\]

\[
r_s=\frac{2GM}{c^2}.
\]

The last relation is a preview of the exact solution, not a derivation from the weak-field approximation.
