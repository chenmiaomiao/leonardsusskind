# Visual Notes

## Selection Standard

Frames were chosen only when the board adds a diagram, an equation state, or an index pattern needed by the surrounding argument. Five neighboring captures were compared around each candidate timestamp. Incidental opening footage and frames dominated by the lecturer's body were rejected where a clearer neighbor existed.

## Visual Sequence

- Figures 1--3 establish intrinsic geometry, a scalar field, and mutually inverse coordinate maps.
- Figures 4--9 build the oblique-basis picture through vector expansion, projection, the Gram matrix, norm, and polar-coordinate scale factors.
- Figures 10--14 preserve the contravariant, covariant, mixed, and rank-two transformation laws and the matching-index tensor equation.
- Figures 15--17 show the operation list, addition, and tensor product.
- Figures 18--23 preserve the inverse-Jacobian lemma and every stage of the contraction argument, including the failed same-variance contraction.
- Figures 24--27 return to the line element, count symmetric metric components, prove the metric transformation law, and display the metric matrix.
- Figures 28--31 show the inverse metric, tangent-space diagram, mixed identity, and explicit two-dimensional matrix product.

All retained images are full-resolution captures from the source video and are placed at the corresponding point in the chapter.
