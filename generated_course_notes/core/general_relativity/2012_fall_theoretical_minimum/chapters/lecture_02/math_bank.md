# Mathematical Spine

## Coordinate Maps

\[
y^m=y^m(x), \qquad x^m=x^m(y), \qquad S'(y)=S(x).
\]

## Basis and Metric

\[
\mathbf V=V^m\mathbf e_m,\qquad
V_m=\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf e_m,\qquad
g_{mn}=\mathbf e_m\cdot\mathbf e_n,
\]
\[
V_m=g_{mn}V^n,\qquad
\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf V=g_{mn}V^mV^n.
\]

## Transformation Laws

\[
V'^m=\frac{\partial y^m}{\partial x^n}V^n,\qquad
W'_m=\frac{\partial x^n}{\partial y^m}W_n,
\]
\[
T'^m{}_n=
\frac{\partial y^m}{\partial x^p}
\frac{\partial x^q}{\partial y^n}T^p{}_q.
\]

## Contraction

\[
\frac{\partial x^b}{\partial y^m}
\frac{\partial y^m}{\partial x^a}
=\delta^b{}_a,
\]
\[
T'^m{}_m=
\frac{\partial y^m}{\partial x^a}
\frac{\partial x^b}{\partial y^m}T^a{}_b
=T^a{}_a.
\]

## Metric

\[
ds^2=g_{mn}(x)\,dx^m dx^n,\qquad
g_{mn}=g_{nm},\qquad
N_g=\frac{d(d+1)}{2},
\]
\[
g'_{pq}(y)=
\frac{\partial x^m}{\partial y^p}
\frac{\partial x^n}{\partial y^q}g_{mn}(x),
\]
\[
g_{mn}g^{np}=\delta_m{}^p.
\]

The metric is positive definite in Riemannian geometry and indefinite but nondegenerate in Lorentzian geometry. A null vector has zero quadratic norm without lying in the kernel of the metric.
