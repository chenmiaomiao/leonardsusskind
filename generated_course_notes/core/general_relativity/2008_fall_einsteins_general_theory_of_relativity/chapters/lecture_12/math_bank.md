# Mathematical Bank

## Conventions

- Spacetime signature: \(+---\).
- Units: \(c=1\).
- Schwarzschild radius: \(r_{\mathrm s}=2GM\).
- Unit-sphere metric: \(d\Omega^2=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2\).

## Core Relations

- Rindler map: \(X=\rho\cosh\omega\), \(T=\rho\sinh\omega\).
- Rindler metric: \(ds^2=\rho^2d\omega^2-d\rho^2-dy^2-dz^2\).
- Fixed-\(\rho\) proper time and acceleration: \(d\tau=\rho\,d\omega\), \(a=1/\rho\).
- Schwarzschild metric:
  \(ds^2=(1-2GM/r)dt^2-dr^2/(1-2GM/r)-r^2d\Omega^2\).
- Curvature invariant: \(R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=48G^2M^2/r^6\).
- Near-horizon proper distance:
  \(\rho=\sqrt{8GM(r-2GM)}\).
- Rindler time and surface gravity:
  \(\omega=t/(4GM)\), \(\kappa=1/(4GM)\).
- Vacuum equations:
  \(G_{\mu\nu}=R_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac12g_{\mu\nu}R=0\).
- Newtonian exterior:
  \(\nabla^2\Phi=0\), \(\Phi=-GM/r\).
- Tidal scale: \(GM/r^3\); at the horizon it scales as \(1/(8G^2M^2)\).
- Mean-density threshold:
  \(\bar\rho_{\mathrm{BH}}=3/(32\pi G^3M^2)\).
- De Sitter scale:
  \(H^2=8\pi G\rho_{\mathrm{vac}}/3\), \(R_{\mathrm{dS}}=H^{-1}\).
- Kerr bound: \(|J|\leq GM^2\), \(|a_*|=|J|/(GM^2)\leq1\).

## Corrections and Qualifications

- A divergent metric component need not imply divergent curvature.
- Schwarzschild time to the horizon is infinite; infalling proper time is finite.
- Classical redshift does not by itself impose a maximum wavelength.
- The near-horizon Rindler approximation says nothing about the \(r=0\) singularity.
- Inside the horizon, fixed \(r\) is spacelike and cannot describe a stationary observer.
- Liouville phase-space incompressibility is classical; quantum mechanics adds a physical cell scale and quantum statistics.
- A negative formal areal radius is not a regular continuation through the curvature singularity.
- The exterior thin-shell picture depends on the Schwarzschild slicing.
