# Visual Review Notes

## Accepted Frame Set

The chapter uses 19 distinct 640 by 480 lecture frames selected from a full-video survey and inspected at their exact extraction times. The set covers:

- the Rindler coordinate map, null rays, proper-time clocks, and nonrigid equal-acceleration hyperbolae;
- the Schwarzschild metric, a manufactured flat-space coordinate singularity, the horizon extension, and the tangent-plane approximation;
- the proper-distance derivation, exact near-horizon Rindler reduction, redshifted signals, and freely falling companions;
- the spacelike singularity, vacuum field equations, mean-density scaling, and loss of causal contact;
- the meter-stick and reflector experiments and the exterior thin-shell description.

## Rejections

- Institutional opening and closing cards.
- Stanford campus and introductory talking-head frames.
- Frames in which the needed equation was incomplete or being erased.
- Duplicate horizon diagrams that added no mathematical or causal evidence.
- Wide shots with an unreadable board when a nearby exact-timestamp frame was available.
- Attractive but topically unrelated images, including the opening campus scene.
