# Lecture 5 Visual Review

## Survey

The complete 01:52:15 video was sampled every 15 seconds. Twenty-three
survey sheets covered the full lecture, followed by exact extraction of
the selected board states.

## Selection Rules

- Retain a frame only when its equation or diagram is used by adjacent text.
- Prefer a complete stable board state over the first moment an equation appears.
- Reject duplicated states, title cards, audience-only shots, active erasing, and avoidable occlusion.
- Keep temporary-sign boards only where their relation \(C=-\Gamma\) is stated explicitly.
- Use the original 320 by 240 source frame without invented crop content or diagram replacement.
