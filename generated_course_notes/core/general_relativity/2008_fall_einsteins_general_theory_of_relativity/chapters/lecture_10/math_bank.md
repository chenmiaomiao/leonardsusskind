# Mathematical Bank

## Conventions

- Metric signature: (+---).
- Einstein equation: G_mn + Lambda g_mn = (8 pi G / c^4) T_mn.
- Local inertial coordinates satisfy g_mn(P) = eta_mn and first derivatives of g vanish at P.

## Core Relations

- Dimensional force law: F_d(r) is proportional to r raised to minus (d minus 1).
- Einstein tensor: G_mn = R_mn - one half g_mn R.
- Contracted Bianchi identity: covariant divergence of G vanishes.
- Metric compatibility: covariant derivative of g vanishes.
- Weak field: g_00 is approximately 1 + 2 Phi / c squared.
- Newtonian limit: Laplacian of Phi equals 4 pi G rho.
- Flat scalar equation: eta raised mn partial_m partial_n phi = 0.
- Curved scalar equation: Box_g phi = one over square root minus g times partial_m of square root minus g times g raised mn partial_n phi = 0.
- Free scalar stress tensor: T_mn = partial_m phi partial_n phi - one half metric_mn times partial_a phi partial raised a phi.
- Scalar energy density: T_00 = one half times time derivative squared plus gradient magnitude squared.
- Isotropic rest-frame stress tensor: covariant components are diag(rho,p,p,p); mixed components are diag(rho,-p,-p,-p).
- Cosmological potential: Phi_Lambda = -Lambda c squared r squared / 6.
- Cosmological acceleration: a_Lambda = Lambda c squared r vector / 3.
- Vacuum stress: T_mn(Lambda) = -Lambda g_mn / (8 pi G) for c = 1.
- Schwarzschild horizon: r_s = 2GM/c squared.
- Photon sphere: r_ph = 3GM/c squared = 3r_s/2.
- Four-dimensional vacuum with Lambda under the chosen convention: R = 4 Lambda and R_mn = Lambda g_mn.

## Corrections and Qualifications

- A vacuum exterior with Lambda zero can have R_mn = 0 while the full Riemann tensor remains nonzero.
- The photon sphere is three halves of the horizon radius, not three horizon radii.
- The sign of the traced Lambda equation follows the curvature and field-equation convention.
- The light-light force estimate is dimensional intuition only; null beams require their full stress-energy tensor.
