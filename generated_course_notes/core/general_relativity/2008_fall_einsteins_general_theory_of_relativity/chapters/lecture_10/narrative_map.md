# Narrative Map

## Sequence

1. Empty flat space receives one point mass. The audience supplies the cone, making curvature and global topology tangible.
2. The deficit angle grows with mass and can close the cone. This turns dimensionality into a physical constraint.
3. Gauss-law scaling in higher dimensions destabilizes orbits, atoms, and nuclear binding. Questions about extra time directions and the status of time return the discussion to special relativity.
4. A cosmological-constant question initiates the tensor argument. Covariant conservation fixes the Einstein tensor, while metric compatibility permits Lambda times the metric.
5. The lecture pauses over epistemic status: Einstein's equation is a constrained guess that must recover Poisson gravity and pass experiment.
6. The equivalence principle is recast as a construction rule. Straight lines become geodesics; a scalar wave becomes the worked field example.
7. The one-dimensional wave equation grows into its three-dimensional and Minkowski forms. A deliberately naive curved-space substitution fails because an ordinary derivative of a vector is not a tensor.
8. Covariant differentiation and the Leibniz rule produce the curved d'Alembertian. Geometry now controls wave propagation.
9. The arrow reverses. Quadratic field derivatives are assembled into a rank-two tensor, and conservation fixes the minus one-half coefficient.
10. The 00 component yields positive kinetic and gradient energy. Pressure, shear, momentum density, and energy flux enlarge the meaning of gravitational source.
11. Localized stress-energy is separated from a nonzero vacuum field. Light's self-gravity shows why exact linear superposition fails in the coupled theory.
12. Lambda becomes a constant source in the Newtonian equation, a quadratic potential, and a linear relative acceleration with no preferred center.
13. Moving Lambda across the equation gives a vacuum stress tensor. Einstein's static balance is shown to sit at an unstable potential maximum.
14. Questions about global rotation, shear axes, spherical solutions, and photon orbits prepare the Schwarzschild calculation.
15. Stress-tensor eigenvalues, the vacuum trace with Lambda, finite cosmologies, and the distinction between spatial and spacetime flatness close the lecture.

## Voice and Rhythm

The prose begins from concrete physical pictures, lets questions expose the next obstacle, and only then compresses the result into tensor notation. Standard refinements are introduced where they repair a sign, coefficient, or distinction, but they do not replace the order of the lecture. The final text reads as prepared lecture notes rather than commentary on a production process.
