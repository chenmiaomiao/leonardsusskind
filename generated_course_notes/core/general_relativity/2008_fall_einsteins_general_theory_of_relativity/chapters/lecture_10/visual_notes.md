# Visual Review Notes

## Accepted Frame Set

The chapter uses 23 distinct 640 by 480 lecture frames. Each frame was selected from a fifteen-second full-video contact-sheet survey and then inspected at its exact extraction time. The sequence covers:

- conical defect and dimensional force-law comparison;
- Einstein tensor, conservation identities, cosmological term, and Newtonian limit;
- flat and curved scalar wave equations;
- scalar stress-tensor ansatz, conservation derivation, final tensor, and energy density;
- isotropic pressure, reciprocal coupling, and localized source;
- Newtonian cosmological-constant equations, linear expansion, and unstable equilibrium;
- shear in rotated axes, photon orbit, and the vacuum trace with Lambda.

## Rejections

- Institutional opening and closing cards.
- Talking-head frames without new board evidence.
- Local-flatness frames whose board still displayed unrelated earlier equations.
- Duplicated, occluded, incomplete, or softer versions of an accepted board state.
- Transcript-corrupted claims about detailed frequency change that were not visually supported.

## PDF Review

All 34 pages were rendered and inspected. Board crops retain their equations and diagrams, captions remain with the intended images, running headers clear the horizontal rule, and no displayed equation crosses the text boundary.
