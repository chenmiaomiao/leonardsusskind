# Mathematical Bank

## Conventions

- Spacetime signature: \(+---\).
- Timelike line element: \(ds^2=c^2d\tau^2\).
- Rindler map: \(X=\rho\cosh\eta\), \(cT=\rho\sinh\eta\).
- Spherical angles: \(d\Omega^2=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2\).

## Core Relations

- Gauss--Bonnet: \(\int_{\Sigma_g}K\,dA=2\pi\chi=4\pi(1-g)\).
- Normal coordinates at \(P\): \(g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}\), \(\partial_\alpha g_{\mu\nu}=0\), and \(\Gamma^\rho_{\mu\nu}=0\).
- Curvature structure: \(R\sim\partial\Gamma+\Gamma^2\sim\partial^2g+(\partial g)^2\).
- Affine geodesic: \(d^2x^\mu/d\tau^2+\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}u^\alpha u^\beta=0\).
- Free-particle action: \(S=-mc^2\int d\tau\).
- Coordinate-time Lagrangian:
  \(L=-mc^2\sqrt{g_{00}+2g_{0i}v^i/c+g_{ij}v^iv^j/c^2}\).
- Weak field: \(g_{00}\simeq1+2\Phi/c^2\).
- Constant proper acceleration:
  \(X^2-c^2T^2=\rho^2\), \(a=c^2/\rho\).
- Rindler metric:
  \(ds^2=\rho^2d\eta^2-d\rho^2-dy^2-dz^2\).
- Fixed inertial position in Rindler coordinates:
  \(\rho(\eta)=X_0/\cosh\eta\).
- Unit sphere:
  \(d\Omega^2=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2\).
- Newtonian potential: \(\Phi=-GM/r\).
- Schwarzschild radius: \(r_s=2GM/c^2\).
- Schwarzschild metric:
  \(ds^2=(1-r_s/r)c^2dt^2-dr^2/(1-r_s/r)-r^2d\Omega^2\).

## Corrections and Qualifications

- Vanishing integrated curvature is necessary but not sufficient for a particular metric to be pointwise flat.
- The punctured sphere is flat only under the pulled-back plane metric, not under the round metric.
- Digit interleaving compares cardinalities but is not a continuous coordinate map.
- A geodesic is stationary and need not be globally shortest or unique.
- Coordinate time is generally nonaffine; its extra tangent term is not a force.
- A Born-rigid Rindler congruence has position-dependent proper acceleration.
- The Rindler horizon carries no curvature and depends on persistent acceleration.
- The radial Schwarzschild coefficient is not fixed by the Newtonian potential alone.
