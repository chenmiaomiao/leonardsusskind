# Narrative Map

## Sequence

1. Positive and negative curvature on a torus cancel globally, and Gauss--Bonnet converts the pattern into a genus invariant.
2. A zero total permits but does not force local flatness. The flat torus separates intrinsic metric from an embedding.
3. Stereographic projection identifies the punctured sphere with the plane. Pulling back the Euclidean metric pushes the deleted point to infinite distance.
4. Digit interleaving shows that equal cardinality does not preserve continuity, differentiability, dimension, or geometry.
5. Normal coordinates remove first metric derivatives at one event. Curvature remains in second derivatives.
6. The hill example replaces the shortest-path slogan with stationarity and admits multiple stable and unstable geodesics.
7. Proper time supplies the particle action. Splitting the metric by coordinate time exposes the general relativistic Lagrangian and the affine-parameter issue.
8. The slow-motion expansion identifies the weak \(g_{00}\) perturbation with twice the Newtonian potential.
9. Constant proper acceleration replaces the Newtonian parabola by a Lorentz-invariant hyperbola.
10. A family of hyperbolae forms a rigid accelerated frame only when proper acceleration varies across the family.
11. Released inertial rocks and light signals expose an observer-dependent, one-way horizon in flat spacetime.
12. Hyperbolic coordinates turn the picture into the exact Rindler metric.
13. A fixed inertial worldline reaches the horizon at finite proper time and infinite Rindler time, explaining the outside redshift without creating a local singularity.
14. Birkhoff's theorem reduces the spherical vacuum exterior to one static family.
15. Standard spherical coordinates and the Newtonian weak-field limit motivate the Schwarzschild time component.
16. The exact radial reciprocal factor completes the exterior metric and prepares the near-horizon Rindler comparison.

## Voice and Rhythm

The prose follows the lecture's alternation between geometric pictures, audience objections, and equations. Mathematical refinements repair live sign or notation drift without replacing the order of explanation. The chapter reads as prepared lecture notes: direct, physically motivated, and complete, without commentary about its production.
