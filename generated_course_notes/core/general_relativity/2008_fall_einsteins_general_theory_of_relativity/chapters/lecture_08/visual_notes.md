# Lecture 8 Visual Notes

## Survey Method

The full 01:46:12 video was sampled every fifteen seconds. Candidate frames
were then extracted at the completed state of each major board argument.
Twenty-two frames survive the content, legibility, obstruction, and timestamp
checks.

  a matrix-valued addition to an ordinary derivative.

## Interpretation Rules

The blackboard is treated as evidence of the lecture sequence, not as an
infallible typeset source. The camera resolution and live index corrections
make some symbols ambiguous. Adjacent equations use one declared Riemann
convention and one metric signature. The low-resolution images remain in the
book because they preserve the original derivational rhythm and spatial
layout.

The sheet-of-paper demonstration at 01:09 is described in prose rather than
used as a figure: it is physically clear in motion but not informative as a
single still frame.
