# Editorial Analysis

## Scope

The chapter follows the complete 107-minute lecture. It preserves the opening
dark-energy exchange, the Newtonian field formalism, both spherical
calculations, the extended classroom dispute over the interior field, the
accelerated-coordinate derivation, light bending, local tidal limitations,
and the closing metric preview.

## Editorial Decisions

- Use direct lecture-note exposition rather than commentary about how notes
  were produced.
- Keep all substantive questions and answers in dedicated classroom blocks.
- Separate physical Gauss law from mathematical Gauss theorem.
- Define displacement and radial sign conventions before using them.
- Preserve live errors as learning moments while typesetting only corrected
  equations.
- Retain the rough physical light-deflection argument and identify precisely
  where the full theory changes it.
- Treat the final geometry section as a preview rather than importing later
  tensor machinery.

## Result

All 35 chronological movements are represented, including eleven focused
classroom exchanges. Fourteen source frames support the equations and
diagrams at their original timestamps.
