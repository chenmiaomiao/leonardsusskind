# Mathematical Record

## Transcript and Board Results

- Weak repulsive model:
  \(\ F_{\mathrm{total}}=-kr+\lambda r\).
- Del operator:
  \(\nabla=(\partial_x,\partial_y,\partial_z)\).
- Gradient and divergence:
  \(\nabla\phi\) and
  \(\nabla\cdot\vec v=\partial_xv_x+\partial_yv_y+\partial_zv_z\).
- Source-directed displacement:
  \(\vec R_i=\vec x_i-\vec x\).
- Discrete field:
  \(\vec A=G\sum_i m_i\vec R_i/R_i^3\).
- Density:
  \(\rho=\lim_{\Delta V\to0}\Delta m/\Delta V\).
- Gravitational Gauss law:
  \(\nabla\cdot\vec A=-4\pi G\rho\).
- Divergence theorem:
  \(\int_V\nabla\cdot\vec A\,dV
  =\oint_{\partial V}\vec A\cdot\hat n\,d\sigma\).
- Spherical field:
  \(A_r=-GM_{\mathrm{enc}}(r)/r^2\).
- Uniform sphere:
  \(M_{\mathrm{enc}}=(4\pi/3)\rho r^3\) and
  \(A_r=-(4\pi G\rho/3)r\).
- Galilean transformation:
  \(x'=x-vt,\ t'=t\).
- Accelerated transformation:
  \(x'=x-\tfrac12gt^2\), hence \(\ddot x'=-g\).
- Elementary light-deflection estimate:
  \(\theta_{\mathrm{Newton}}\approx2GM/(Rc^2)\).
- General two-dimensional metric:
  \(ds^2=g_{xx}dx^2+2g_{xy}dx\,dy+g_{yy}dy^2\).

## Standard Completions

- Uniform-Earth oscillator:
  \(\omega^2=4\pi G\rho/3=GM/R^3=g_{\mathrm s}/R\).
- Uniform-Earth period:
  \(T=2\pi\sqrt{R/g_{\mathrm s}}\approx84\) minutes, giving roughly
  \(42\) minutes from surface to opposite surface.
- Full weak-field solar deflection:
  \(\theta_{\mathrm{GR}}=4GM/(Rc^2)\), twice the elementary estimate.
- A longitudinal gravitational effect changes photon energy and frequency,
  not the locally measured speed \(c\).

## Source Corrections

- Divergence is a scalar even though one sentence calls it a vector.
- The interior field retains the full \(4\pi G\rho/3\) coefficient.
- Dividing an \(r^3\) enclosed mass by an \(r^2\) area produces a field
  proportional to \(r\), not \(1/r\).
- The uncertain ten-minute tunnel estimate is replaced by the value implied
  by the stated uniform-density model.
- The full relativistic deflection is twice, not half, the elementary result.
