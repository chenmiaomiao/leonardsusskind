# Lecture 4 Visual Verification

## Selection Rule

The full recording was surveyed at fifteen-second intervals. Adjacent
candidates were then compared around every mathematical transition. The
retained frame had to show a complete equation or a diagram used directly
in the chapter.

## Rejections

- Institutional opening and closing cards contain no lecture argument.
- Incomplete Jacobian, inverse-metric, and cosmological equations were
  rejected in favor of later complete boards.
- Repeated inverse-metric views were reduced to distinct mathematical
  stages rather than retained as near-duplicates.
- The board's transient equality in the displacement contraction was not
  selected; the retained frame follows the spoken correction.
- The cosmological frame documents the scale-factor idea, while the chapter
  uses one consistent convention for the squared spatial coefficient.
