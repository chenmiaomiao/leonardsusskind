## Selection Rule

The full lecture was surveyed at regular intervals, followed by
frame-by-frame comparison around each retained timestamp. A frame was kept
only when its board content directly supports the surrounding derivation or
diagram.

## Rejections

- Institutional opening and closing cards contain no lecture content.
- Earlier candidates at several timestamps show equations still being
  written or erased.
- Redundant metric frames were removed when one complete board preserved the
  same derivation.
- The final spoken sphere formula is typeset from the verified geometric
  setup because the video cuts to the closing card before a clean completed
  formula remains on screen.
