# Lecture 7 Narrative Map

## 1. Local Curvature from Holonomy

Review parallel transport around a small surface loop, preserve the question
about locality, and distinguish intrinsic measurements from an embedding.
Retain the wheeled bug and gyroscope as operational pictures.

## 2. Two Planes in Higher Dimensions

Separate the plane of the infinitesimal loop from the plane in which a vector
or orthonormal frame rotates. Preserve the perpendicularity question and
answer it with metric compatibility.

## 3. The Infinitesimal Rectangle

Recover the pathwise transport equation, follow the four sides of a closed
loop, and identify the leading vector change as bilinear in the edge vectors.
Correct the live index collision explicitly.

## 4. Riemann as a Tensor

State one sign convention, reconstruct the full component formula, and
explain tensoriality through the closed-loop multilinear map. Recast the
quadratic connection terms as a matrix commutator.

## 5. Normal Coordinates and Symmetries

Correct the second-derivative remark, lower the output index, state the pair
symmetries and Bianchi identity, and give the corrected component count.
Preserve questions about orientation, sphere radius, and loop closure.

## 6. Ricci Contraction

Contract one index from each pair, establish Ricci symmetry, and show exactly
why Ricci determines Riemann in three dimensions but not in four.

## 7. Charge as a Warm-Up

Define charge density, draw the world-tube and worldlines, define current
through an oriented window, and combine density and current into \(J^\mu\).

## 8. Local Conservation

Move from boundary flux to the continuity equation. Preserve the distinction
between global bookkeeping and the local prohibition against disappearance
here followed by reappearance elsewhere.

## 9. Scalar Charge and the Capacitor

Explain why total charge is Lorentz invariant while density and current mix.
Retain the extended capacitor exchange with corrected charge and energy
relations.

## 10. Energy, Momentum, and Stress

Assemble four-momentum, interpret every block of \(T^{\mu\nu}\), and retain
the particle-flux picture behind off-diagonal stresses. Explain rather than
assume tensoriality and symmetry.

## 11. Four Conservation Laws

Write the row-by-row continuity equations, compress them into
\(\partial_\mu T^{\nu\mu}=0\), and then covariantize the divergence.

## 12. Geodesics and the Newtonian Limit

Close the matter-curvature-motion chain with the geodesic equation. Preserve
the audience identification of the large time component and derive
\(d^2x^i/dt^2\simeq-\Gamma^i{}_{00}\).
