# Lecture 6 Mathematics

## Proper Time

\[
d\tau^2=g_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu,
\qquad
\eta_{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1).
\]

\[
d\tau^2=dt^2-\frac{dx^2+dy^2+dz^2}{c^2},
\qquad
d\tau=dt\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}.
\]

## Local Inertial Coordinates

\[
g_{mn}(p)=\delta_{mn},
\qquad
\partial_r g_{mn}(p)=0,
\qquad
\Gamma^r{}_{mn}(p)=0.
\]

\[
y^m=x^m+C^m{}_{rs}x^r x^s+O(x^3),
\qquad
N_C=\frac{D^2(D+1)}{2}.
\]

\[
\Gamma^r{}_{mn}
=
\frac12g^{rs}
\left(
\partial_mg_{sn}
+\partial_ng_{sm}
-\partial_sg_{mn}
\right),
\qquad
\nabla_rg_{mn}=0.
\]

## Curves and Transport

\[
t^p=\frac{dx^p}{ds},
\qquad
\frac{Dv^m}{Ds}
=
\frac{dv^m}{ds}
+\Gamma^m{}_{np}v^n\frac{dx^p}{ds}.
\]

\[
\frac{d^2x^m}{ds^2}
+\Gamma^m{}_{np}
\frac{dx^n}{ds}
\frac{dx^p}{ds}
=0
\]
for an affine geodesic parameter.

\[
dv^m=-\Gamma^m{}_{np}v^n\,dx^p,
\qquad
\frac{D}{Ds}\bigl(g_{mn}u^m v^n\bigr)=0
\]
for parallel-transported \(u\) and \(v\).

## Curvature from Holonomy

\[
\Delta\alpha=\Theta,
\qquad
\int_\Sigma K\,dA=\Theta
\]
for a loop enclosing the apex of a cone with angular deficit \(\Theta\).

\[
\delta\alpha=K(p)\,\delta A+O(\delta A^{3/2}),
\qquad
K_{\text{sphere}}=\frac{1}{\rho^2}.
\]

\[
\int_{\mathbb T^2}K\,dA=0
\]
for the torus, although \(K\) need not vanish pointwise.
